# When a Federal Holiday Falls on a Weekend, What Changes?

**For most federal employees, a federal holiday that falls on a Saturday is treated as a holiday on the Friday before, and one that falls on a Sunday is treated as a holiday on the Monday after.** This is the rule the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM) publishes for federal employees.

This article explains that rule and shows the dates in OPM's tables for 2026 to 2028. The facts are as of October 7, 2026, and come from the two sources listed at the end. It is about federal employees only, not about banks, schools, post offices or private employers.

## The rule

OPM says most federal employees work a Monday through Friday schedule. For them, it says, a holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday usually is observed on the Friday before or the Monday after. OPM's footnote says that for most federal employees the preceding Friday "will be treated as a holiday for pay and leave purposes" when the holiday falls on a Saturday, and the following Monday when it falls on a Sunday.

## Where the rule comes from

For Saturday, OPM cites 5 U.S.C. 6103(b). That section says that instead of a holiday that occurs on a Saturday, the Friday immediately before is a legal public holiday for employees whose basic workweek is Monday through Friday. It applies to statutes on pay and leave.

For Sunday, OPM cites Section 3(a) of Executive Order 11582 (February 11, 1971). The order is printed in the notes to the statute. It says that an employee whose basic workweek does not include Sunday, and who would ordinarily be excused from work on a holiday, is excused on the next workday when a holiday falls on a Sunday.

## Examples in OPM's tables

OPM's tables mark these dates with a footnote. The weekday of each actual holiday date is computed from the calendar.

| Holiday | Falls on | OPM lists |
|---|---|---|
| Independence Day 2026 | Saturday, July 4, 2026 | Friday, July 3, 2026 |
| Juneteenth 2027 | Saturday, June 19, 2027 | Friday, June 18, 2027 |
| Independence Day 2027 | Sunday, July 4, 2027 | Monday, July 5, 2027 |
| Christmas Day 2027 | Saturday, December 25, 2027 | Friday, December 24, 2027 |
| New Year's Day 2028 | Saturday, January 1, 2028 | Friday, December 31, 2027 |

OPM lists the New Year's Day 2028 row under its 2028 tab, but the date it shows falls in 2027.

The three federal holidays still ahead in 2026 are Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, November 26, and Christmas Day on Friday, December 25. All three fall on weekdays, so OPM's 2026 table lists them on their own dates.

## The limits of the rule

The rule is for federal employees. OPM says it applies to most of them, and the statute has separate rules for employees whose basic workweek is not Monday through Friday. OPM also has a fact sheet titled "Federal Holidays - 'In Lieu Of' Determination," which this article does not summarize.

## What this article does not cover

This article does not cover what state or local governments, banks, schools, the Postal Service or private employers do when a holiday falls on a weekend. Each of them sets its own schedule, and this article uses no source for any of them. It also does not cover pay and leave details, other work schedules, or Inauguration Day. This article describes what the two sources state on the date above and does not track changes to them.
