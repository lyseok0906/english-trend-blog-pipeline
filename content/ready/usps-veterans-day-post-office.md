# Is the Post Office Open on Veterans Day? What USPS Says for 2026

**USPS says it will observe Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, and that all Post Office locations will be closed.** The notice adds that regular mail delivery and retail services will resume on Thursday, November 12. Both statements come from a USPS national release dated November 4, 2026.

This article covers only what that release says about November 11 and 12, 2026. The wording is as of November 6, 2026. It does not cover other carriers, local Post Office hours or other holidays.

## What the USPS notice says

The release is titled "U.S. Postal Service To Observe Veterans Day Holiday, Nov. 11" and is published in the USPS Newsroom. This is what it states about the holiday:

| Date | What USPS says |
|---|---|
| Wednesday, November 11, 2026 | USPS will observe Veterans Day. All Post Office locations will be closed. |
| Thursday, November 12, 2026 | Regular mail delivery and retail services will resume. |

## What the notice does not say

The notice does not say whether Priority Mail Express will be delivered on November 11, either that it will or that it will not. This article does not fill that gap from other years or from other USPS notices, so on that question the notice gives no answer.

The notice also does not list hours for any individual Post Office. Apart from the date on which regular mail delivery and retail services resume, it does not describe delivery on November 11.
