# Can You Bring Deodorant on a Plane? What TSA Says by Type

**Yes, in both carry-on and checked bags, with size limits for sprays and liquids.** TSA's pages list solid deodorant as allowed in carry-on and checked bags and show no size limit. For aerosol deodorant and liquid deodorant, TSA lists carry-on bags as "Yes" with 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less allowed, and checked bags as "Yes."

This article covers what three TSA "What Can I Bring?" pages say (Deodorant (Solid), Deodorant (aerosol) and Deodorant (liquid)) and what the FAA rule they point to, 49 CFR 175.10(a)(1), says about toiletries in checked bags. The wording is as of November 9, 2026. Two of the pages carry a 2023 date and one a 2017 date, and the article gives each date. It does not cover other toiletries, gel or roll-on products, antiperspirant, airline rules, other countries or any one brand.

## The three pages in one table

| Type of deodorant | TSA: carry-on bags | TSA: checked bags | What the page adds | Page date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solid | Yes | Yes | No size limit or other instruction on the page | March 30, 2017 |
| Aerosol | Yes (3.4 oz / 100 ml or less allowed) | Yes | Checked-bag totals; release device must be protected | March 29, 2023 |
| Liquid | Yes (3.4 oz / 100 ml or less allowed) | Yes | Same text as the aerosol page | March 29, 2023 |

## Solid deodorant

TSA's Deodorant (Solid) page lists carry-on bags "Yes" and checked bags "Yes." It shows no size limit, no checked-bag totals and no other instruction. It is the oldest of the three pages (last updated March 30, 2017).

## Aerosol and liquid deodorant in carry-on

TSA's Deodorant (aerosol) and Deodorant (liquid) pages carry the same header and the same text. Both list carry-on bags as "Yes (Less than or equal to 3.4oz/100 ml allowed)" and checked bags as "Yes." Both were last updated on March 29, 2023. The pages do not explain the 3.4-ounce figure, and this article does not add rules that the pages do not state. TSA's general liquids rule is covered in [What Is the TSA 3-1-1 Liquids Rule? What's Exempt?](https://statesideexplained.com/tsa-3-1-1-liquids-rule/).

## Aerosol and liquid deodorant in a checked bag

The aerosol and liquid pages say the FAA limits the total amount of restricted medicinal and toiletry articles, including aerosols, in checked baggage. The total aggregate quantity per person cannot exceed 2 kg (70 ounces) or 2 L (68 fluid ounces), and the capacity of each container must not exceed 0.5 kg (18 ounces) or 500 ml (17 fluid ounces). The pages end that paragraph with "See the FAA regulations for more information."

The FAA rule these pages point to appears to be 49 CFR 175.10(a)(1), which the eCFR shows as up to date as of November 5, 2026. It covers non-radioactive medicinal and toilet articles for personal use, including aerosols, carried in carry-on and checked baggage, and it states the same aggregate quantity (2 kg or 2 L per person) and the same container capacity (0.5 kg or 500 ml). It also says the release of gas must not cause extreme annoyance or discomfort to crew members so as to prevent the correct performance of assigned duties. The TSA pages do not cite this paragraph by number, so this article does not say that TSA applies exactly this text.

## Release devices on aerosols

Both the aerosol and the liquid page say permitted aerosol release devices (button or nozzle) must be protected by caps or other suitable means to prevent accidental release. 49 CFR 175.10(a)(1) says the release devices on aerosols must be protected by a cap or other suitable means to prevent inadvertent release. The Solid page does not mention release devices.

## What the pages do not say

TSA's What Can I Bring? item search for "deodorant" returns exactly these three pages, so there is no separate TSA page for gel deodorant or roll-on deodorant, and this article says nothing about them. None of the pages says how many deodorants you may carry, and none says anything about a brand or ingredients. TSA says on each page that the final decision rests with the TSA officer on whether an item is allowed through the checkpoint.
