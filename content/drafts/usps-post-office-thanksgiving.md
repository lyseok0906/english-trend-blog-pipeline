# Is the Post Office Open on Thanksgiving? What USPS Says in 2026

**USPS says it will observe the Thanksgiving federal holiday on Thursday, November 26, 2026, and that all Post Office locations will be closed.** The notice adds that regular mail delivery and retail services will resume on Friday, November 27. Both statements come from a USPS national release dated November 17, 2026.

This article covers only what that release says about November 26 and 27, 2026, and what it lists for customers while Post Offices are closed. The wording is as of November 18, 2026. It does not cover other carriers, local Post Office hours or other holidays, and it does not use USPS notices from earlier years.

## What the USPS notice says

The release is titled "U.S. Postal Service To Observe Thanksgiving Holiday, Nov. 26" and is published in the USPS Newsroom. This is what it states about the holiday:

| Date | What USPS says |
|---|---|
| Thursday, November 26, 2026 | USPS will observe the Thanksgiving federal holiday. All Post Office locations will be closed. |
| Friday, November 27, 2026 | Regular mail delivery and retail services will resume. |

USPS's "Holidays & events" page also lists Thursday, November 26, 2026 as Thanksgiving Day. That page does not say whether Post Offices are open or closed, so the release is the source for the closure.

## What USPS lists while Post Offices are closed

The release's subtitle says that self-service options are available in many retail lobbies when Post Offices are closed. In the text, USPS reminds customers that on usps.com they can order stamps and free USPS boxes and use services such as Hold Mail, Change of Address, PO Box rental or renewal, and Click-N-Ship to print prepaid labels. Our [Hold Mail article](https://statesideexplained.com/usps-hold-mail-how-it-works/) covers one of those services.

The release also says self-service kiosks are available in select lobbies nationwide. It lists these kiosk features: postage printing for Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and international shipping; stamps for purchase; weighing and mailing packages; and package tracking information. It says that as USPS modernizes its retail locations, more self-service options are becoming available at more locations and may include Rapid Dropoff Stations for customers who have already printed labels, printing a shipping label from a merchant-provided QR code, and USPS Smart Lockers for dropping off and picking up packages. It adds that many kiosks are available 24/7.

The release does not name the lobbies that have kiosks, so it does not tell you whether a particular location has one.

## What the notice does not say

The notice does not say whether Priority Mail Express will be delivered on November 26, either that it will or that it will not. The only place the release mentions Priority Mail Express is the kiosk list above, where it names the postage a kiosk can print. That is not a statement about delivery. This article does not fill the gap from other years or from other USPS notices, so on that question the notice gives no answer.

The notice also does not list hours for any individual Post Office or lobby. Apart from the date on which regular mail delivery and retail services resume, it does not describe delivery on November 26, and it does not give mailing deadlines. USPS publishes its recommended holiday mailing dates separately; our [holiday shipping deadlines article](https://statesideexplained.com/usps-holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/) covers the Christmas dates.
