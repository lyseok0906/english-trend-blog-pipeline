# How Does a Forever Stamp Work? What It Covers and Doesn't

**A Forever stamp is a U.S. Postal Service stamp that, USPS says, is "always valid for the First-Class Mail 1 oz rate, even if postage rates increase."** USPS describes it as a stamp for domestic mail weighing up to 1 ounce.

This article explains what USPS's own pages say. The wording below is as of October 7, 2026, and comes from the four USPS pages listed at the end. None of the four pages shows an update date. The price list shows its effective date, October 4, 2026.

## What "Forever" means

USPS's store page for a 2026 Forever stamp says its value is "always valid for the First-Class Mail 1 oz rate, even if postage rates increase." In other words, USPS says the stamp keeps its value for the 1-ounce rate when that rate changes.

USPS's Postal Facts site says the first Forever stamp was issued on April 12, 2007.

## What a Forever stamp covers

USPS's store page says a Forever stamp is for sending domestic mail weighing up to 1 ounce via First-Class Mail. It lists the purpose as sending letters and cards up to 1 ounce within the U.S., including U.S. territories and military bases overseas.

For a sense of scale, the same page says 1 ounce is approximately 4 sheets of regular 8-1/2" x 11" paper in a rectangular envelope.

## What it does not cover

**Mail over 1 ounce.** A Forever stamp is for mail up to 1 ounce. USPS's price list charges more for heavier letters: a stamped letter up to 2 ounces and one up to 3 ounces each have a higher price than a stamped letter up to 1 ounce. This article does not cover how to add postage for a heavier letter.

**International mail.** USPS's store page describes a Forever stamp as being for domestic mail. USPS's international page describes the Global Forever stamp for sending 1-ounce letters or postcards to other countries. It says that stamp never expires, even if the postage price goes up, and that First-Class Mail International reaches more than 180 countries. The USPS pages used here do not say whether a regular Forever stamp can be used on international mail, so this article does not say.

## What this article does not cover

This article does not give postage prices, where to buy stamps, stamp designs, postcard rates or how to mail packages. Prices change, so the current price is on USPS's pages. This article describes what USPS's pages state on the date above and does not track changes to them.
