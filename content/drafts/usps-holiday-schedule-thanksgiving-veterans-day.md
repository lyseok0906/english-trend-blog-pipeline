## Veterans Day 2026: what the USPS notice says

The USPS release "U.S. Postal Service To Observe Veterans Day Holiday, Nov. 11" is dated November 4, 2026. It says the Postal Service will observe Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, that all Post Office locations will be closed, and that regular mail delivery and retail services will resume on Thursday, November 12.

The notice does not mention Priority Mail Express delivery on November 11, either to say it will be delivered or to say it will not. USPS's 2025 Veterans Day notice did contain such a sentence (see below), so the 2025 wording should not be read as the 2026 plan.

## What USPS says you can still do when Post Offices are closed

The 2026 Veterans Day notice says that when Post Offices are closed, customers can still visit usps.com to order stamps and free USPS boxes, and to use Hold Mail, Change of Address, PO Box rental or renewal, and Click-N-Ship to print prepaid labels. It also says self-service kiosks are available in select lobbies nationwide, with postage printing for Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and international shipping, stamps for purchase, package weighing and mailing, and package tracking. It adds that many kiosks are available 24/7. [[HOLD-CHECK: repeat only what the 2026 Thanksgiving notice also says.]]

## How USPS's notices have differed from year to year

USPS announces each holiday in its own release, and the wording changes. The 2025 releases are shown here as dated examples, not as 2026 information.

- **Veterans Day 2025 (release of October 29, 2025):** all Post Office locations closed on Tuesday, November 11, 2025; only Priority Mail Express delivered that day; regular mail delivery and retail services resumed Wednesday, November 12.
- **Veterans Day 2026 (release of November 4, 2026):** all Post Office locations closed on Wednesday, November 11; regular delivery and retail services resume Thursday, November 12; no sentence on Priority Mail Express.
- **Thanksgiving 2025 (release of November 13, 2025):** all Post Office locations closed on Thursday, November 27, 2025 and only Priority Mail Express delivered; regular mail delivery and retail services resumed Friday, November 28.

USPS's date also does not always match the federal "observed" date. In its release of June 26, 2026, USPS said it would observe Independence Day on Saturday, July 4, that Post Offices would be closed that day, and that Post Offices would be open and deliveries would occur as normal on Friday, July 3.
