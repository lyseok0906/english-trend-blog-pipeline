# When Is Thanksgiving 2026 in the U.S.? How the Date Is Set

**Thanksgiving 2026 in the U.S. is Thursday, November 26.** Federal law sets Thanksgiving Day as "the fourth Thursday in November," so the date is not the same every year.

This article explains the rule, using two U.S. government sources listed at the end. The facts are as of October 7, 2026. It is about the U.S. holiday, not Canada's.

## The date, year by year

OPM's federal holiday tables list these dates for Thanksgiving Day:

| Year | Thanksgiving Day |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Thursday, November 28 |
| 2025 | Thursday, November 27 |
| 2026 | Thursday, November 26 |
| 2027 | Thursday, November 25 |
| 2028 | Thursday, November 23 |
| 2029 | Thursday, November 22 |
| 2030 | Thursday, November 28 |

Each of these dates is the fourth Thursday of its November.

## How the rule gives November 26

The law's list of legal public holidays reads "Thanksgiving Day, the fourth Thursday in November." November 1, 2026 is a Sunday, so the Thursdays in November 2026 are the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th. The fourth one is the 26th.

## Why the date moves

The first Thursday of any November falls between the 1st and the 7th. The fourth Thursday comes three weeks later, so it always falls between the 22nd and the 28th. That is arithmetic on the law's wording, not a statement from OPM. The table above shows both ends of the range: November 22 in 2029, and November 28 in 2024 and 2030.

## What "federal holiday" means here

OPM says federal law (5 U.S.C. 6103) establishes the public holidays on its pages "for Federal employees." The statute calls them "legal public holidays." This article does not say what private employers, schools, state governments, banks or stores do on that day. Each of them sets its own schedule.

## What this article does not cover

This article does not cover the history of Thanksgiving, traditions, travel, or store and school closings. It does not cover Canada. It does not cover the day after Thanksgiving, which is not on OPM's list of federal holidays. This article describes what the sources state on the date above and does not track changes to them.
