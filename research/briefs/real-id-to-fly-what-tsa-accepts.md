## Topic

What ID TSA says adult passengers need to pass the checkpoint, where REAL ID fits, and what happens without an acceptable ID.

## Working title (proposal)

Do You Need a REAL ID to Fly? What TSA Accepts

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `do i need a real id to fly`
- **Intent:** informational and practical: does my license work, what else works.
- **Demand signal:** not checked by autocomplete (tool unreliable on 2026-10-07). A web search on 2026-10-07 returned state DMV, airport, AAA and travel-site pages. **Search volume and competition were not checked.** Many commercial and local pages exist (titles only; not read).

## Audience

US adult air travelers who are unsure whether their driver's license or another ID will get them through the checkpoint.

## Angle

1. **The answer (TSA).** Adults 18 and older must show valid identification at the checkpoint. A state-issued REAL ID-compliant license or ID is on TSA's list, and so are a passport, a passport card and other listed IDs. TSA says that as of May 7, 2025, state-issued licenses and IDs that are not REAL ID compliant are no longer accepted at airports.
2. **What else works.** TSA lists examples (U.S. passport or passport card, DHS trusted traveler cards, military ID, permanent resident card, Tribal photo ID, and others). The article can show a short list and send the reader to TSA's full list.
3. **Without an acceptable ID.** TSA says that starting February 1, 2026, a traveler who cannot provide an acceptable ID has the option of TSA ConfirmID, a fee-based identity verification; TSA says it "could take 30 minutes or more" and that if identity cannot be verified the traveler will not be allowed into the checkpoint. The fee amount is not used in the article (user decision 2026-10-07).
4. **Details TSA states.** A temporary driver's license is not acceptable; TSA currently accepts expired ID up to two years after expiration for the listed IDs; children under 18 are not required to show ID for domestic travel; certain mobile driver's licenses from approved states are accepted if based on a REAL ID, EDL or EID.

## Why now

REAL ID enforcement began in May 2025 (TSA), so this is an ongoing question rather than a deadline. Holiday air travel starts in about six weeks (Thanksgiving is November 26); interest probably rises then (Claude's judgment, not measured).

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** TSA's acceptable ID statements above; what REAL ID is, as TSA defines it; the ConfirmID option and the consequence of unverified identity.
- **Out:** how to get a REAL ID at a state DMV (varies by state; TSA only points to a state selector), international travel and passports for entering other countries, airline-specific ID rules, federal-facility access, immigration status, digital ID program details beyond naming the approved-state condition, advice such as "get one now".
- Fee: **excluded** (user decision 2026-10-07: not core to the question, and a changing price adds re-check burden). The article may say ConfirmID is fee-based, with no amount.

## Image plan (no photos)

Own flowchart: adult passenger → acceptable ID on TSA's list? → proceed; if not → TSA ConfirmID option (fee-based, may take longer; unverified identity cannot enter the checkpoint). No license or passport images.

## Official source notes (read 2026-10-07 in the built-in browser)

- **TSA, "Acceptable Identification at the TSA Checkpoint"**, https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/identification . "Adult passengers 18 and older must show valid identification at the airport checkpoint in order to travel." "The list of acceptable IDs is subject to change without notice." "As of May 7, 2025, state-issued driver's licenses and IDs that are not REAL ID compliant are no longer accepted as valid forms of identification at airports." "A temporary driver's license is not an acceptable form of identification." "TSA currently accepts expired ID up to two years after expiration". "TSA does not require children under 18 to provide identification when traveling within the United States." "If your identity cannot be verified, you will not be allowed to enter the screening checkpoint." ConfirmID paragraph: "Starting February 1, 2026, if you are unable to provide the required acceptable form of ID at a TSA checkpoint, you will have the option to pay a [fee amount, not used in the article] fee to use TSA ConfirmID." No update date shown.
- **TSA, "REAL ID"**, https://www.tsa.gov/realid . "Enforcement began May 7, 2025." "Starting May 2025, U.S. travelers must be REAL ID compliant to board domestic flights and access certain federal facilities." Describes the REAL ID Act, passed by Congress in 2005. No update date shown.
- **TSA, "About TSA ConfirmID"**, https://www.tsa.gov/tsaconfirm-id/about-confirmid . "TSA ConfirmID is a fee-based service"; "The identification process takes an average of 10-15 minutes; however, it could take 30 minutes or more."; "A REAL ID features a star in the upper right or left corner." (do not draw the star). Lists Federal Register notices dated 11/20/2025 and 12/3/2025. No update date shown.
