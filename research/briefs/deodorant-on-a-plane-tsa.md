## Official sources (read 2026-11-09; see source notes)

Three TSA "What Can I Bring?" pages (Deodorant (aerosol), Deodorant (liquid), Deodorant (Solid)); TSA's item search for "deodorant" returns exactly these three. The aerosol and liquid pages point to "the FAA regulations"; the FAA rule (49 CFR 175.10(a)(1), eCFR up to date as of 11/05/2026) is read as the fourth source. Page dates: March 29, 2023 (aerosol and liquid), March 30, 2017 (solid).

- **In:** what each of the three TSA pages lists for carry-on and checked bags; the 3.4 oz / 100 ml mention on the aerosol and liquid pages; the checked-bag totals from the same pages and 49 CFR 175.10(a)(1); the cap on aerosol release devices; the final-decision line.
