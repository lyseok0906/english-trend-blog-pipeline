## Reader problem (three readers, kept apart)

1. **I received mail that is not mine** (a previous resident, a wrong address, a different person). What do the pages say to do, and what should I not do?
2. **I do not want this mail.** Can I refuse it, before or after delivery, and what are the limits?
3. **I sent mail and it came back, or did not arrive.** What do the pages say about return, no return address, postage, marketing mail and dead mail?
