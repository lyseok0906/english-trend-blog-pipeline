## Topic

What USPS says Informed Delivery is, what it shows (and does not show), how notifications arrive, and how sign-up identity verification works.

## Working title (proposal)

What Is USPS Informed Delivery? How the Free Preview Works

(No apostrophe on purpose: Rank Math's structured data showed `&#039;` for apostrophes in the earlier two titles.)

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Intent:** informational; some readers want to know whether it is safe or how to sign up.

## Audience

US residents who have seen the name, received a USPS letter about it, or want to know what the daily email is.

## Angle

1. **What it is (USPS FAQ).** "a free and optional online notification feature that gives residential, business and eligible PO Box consumers the ability to digitally preview their letter-sized mail and manage their packages scheduled to arrive soon."
2. **How the preview is made (USPS).** USPS "digitally images the front of letter-sized mail that runs through our automated mail sorting equipment" and uses those images for notifications before the physical mail arrives. The images show "the external markings, showing only the exterior, address side of letter-sized mailpieces."
3. **What it shows and does not (USPS).** Daily email: images of up to 10 incoming letters; the dashboard has no limit; some pieces such as catalogs or magazines are not imaged; packages appear as status updates, not images ("Will I see images of packages? No.").
4. **When it arrives (USPS).** "Emails are sent once daily, typically before 9:00AM local time, Monday through Saturday. Notifications are not sent on days when there is no mail to be delivered, on Sundays, or federal holidays." Also on the dashboard and the mobile app.
5. **Who can sign up and how (USPS).** Most residential and PO Box addresses and many business addresses; an address that is not uniquely coded, "especially in high density areas (e.g., apartment buildings or condos)", cannot sign up. Identity is verified; if that cannot be done online, a code arrives by mail "within 3-7 postal business days" (valid 90 days or five uses) or the person verifies in person at a Post Office.
6. **The letter people ask about (USPS).** USPS says a letter about a new sign-up at an address is part of its mail-based verification, and gives the unsubscribe step (usps.com/idremove with the code in the letter).

## Official source notes (read 2026-10-07 in the built-in browser)

- **USPS FAQ, "Informed Delivery - The Basics"**, https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Informed-Delivery-The-Basics . Page date "Sep 30, 2026". "Informed Delivery is a free and optional notification feature that gives residential, business and eligible PO Box consumers the ability to digitally preview their letter-sized mail and manage their packages scheduled to arrive soon." Overview section: "The United States Postal Service (USPS) digitally images the front of letter-sized mail that runs through our automated mail sorting equipment. USPS uses those images to provide digital notifications in advance of the delivery of physical mail." Eligibility: "Informed Delivery is available to most residential and PO Box addresses, and many business addresses." "In rare instances, you may live in an eligible ZIP Code location, but can't sign up because your individual mailbox is not uniquely coded ... most addresses are uniquely coded, not all are, especially in high density areas (e.g., apartment buildings or condos)." "You will receive daily emails ... containing images of the address side of up to 10 pieces of incoming letter mail. You can also view these images on your Informed Delivery online dashboard, where there is no limit on the number of images that will be shown." "Some mailpieces (e.g., catalogs or magazines) are not imaged by our automated equipment and will not appear". "Informed Delivery can only be sent to one email address per account. However, multiple accounts can have Informed Delivery for the same address." "Emails are sent once daily, typically before 9:00AM local time, Monday through Saturday. Notifications are not sent on days when there is no mail to be delivered, on Sundays, or federal holidays." Sign-up: create account, "Verify your identity"; "If your identity cannot be verified online, the system will automatically present options to either 'Request Invitation Code by Mail' or 'Verify at Post Office'"; "You should receive your code in the mail within 3-7 postal business days"; "Code expires 90 days from the date of issue or after 5 successful use". Packages: "Will I see images of packages? No. Package information will be presented as status updates, not images." Outbound packages appear in the email only if the account return address is on the package and it is within three days of expected delivery. Privacy: "I received a letter saying that someone at my home address signed up for Informed Delivery. This letter is sent as a part of our mail-based verification of new Informed Delivery accounts." Steps: visit usps.com/idremove, enter the code from the letter, choose a reason, submit. "Email messages will be sent from USPSInformeddelivery@email.informeddelivery.usps.com". "The scanned mail images are of the external markings, showing only the exterior, address side of letter-sized mailpieces. Package notifications do not include images".
- **USPS, "Informed Delivery - Mail & Package Notifications"**, https://www.usps.com/manage/informed-delivery.htm . Meta description: "Informed Delivery is a free service from USPS that shows you preview images of incoming mail, plus status updates about your incoming and outbound packages. Get notifications in a morning Daily Digest email, or at any time from the dashboard using your smartphone, computer, or USPS Mobile app." Page body (read in full, 7,082 characters): "See Photos of Your Mail Before It Arrives, Free"; "See images of your incoming letter-sized mail (grayscale, address side only)."; three sign-up steps (create account, "Verify Your Identity", watch for updates); "NEW! Enhance Your Account Security: Add multifactor authentication (MFA) to protect your account. It's required for some users and recommended for all."; dashboard functions (package details and tracking history, delivery instructions, schedule a redelivery, nickname, remove a package, track Click-N-Ship packages, add tracking numbers); footnote: "Images are only provided for letter-sized mailpieces that are processed through USPS's automated equipment. Mail and packages may not arrive the same day you get a notification—please allow several days for delivery."; an FAQ list including "How much does Informed Delivery cost?" (answer not captured). Electronic Signature Online, mail delivery notification emails and the mobile app are also described; they are out of scope.
