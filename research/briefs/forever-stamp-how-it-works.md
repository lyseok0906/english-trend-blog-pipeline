## Topic

What a Forever stamp is, what it pays for, and what it does not cover.

## Working title (proposal)

How Does a Forever Stamp Work? What It Covers and What It Doesn't

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `how does a forever stamp work`
- **Intent:** informational.
- **Demand signal:** Google autocomplete (US setting), fetched 2026-10-06, gave 10 suggestions (full list in the source notes). They include `how do forever stamps work usps`, `how much weight does a forever stamp work for`, `does a forever stamp work for international mail`, `does a forever stamp work for canada`, `how does a global forever stamp work`, and `how does costco forever stamps work`. International and retailer-specific intents are mixed in. **Search volume and competition were not checked.**

## Audience

US residents who buy stamps, and anyone unsure whether a stamp bought earlier still works.

## Angle

1. **The name.** A Forever stamp pays the First-Class Mail 1-ounce rate even after postage prices go up (USPS). First issued April 12, 2007 (USPS Postal Facts).
2. **What it covers.** A USPS stamp product page lists it as domestic mail, up to 1 ounce. More weight needs more postage: the USPS price list page showed higher prices for 2 and 3 ounces (read through the tool; see source notes).
3. **What it does not cover.** International mail uses a different stamp, the Global Forever, which USPS describes on its international page as a separate stamp.

Do **not** put a price number in the article text, or give it with an "as of" date. Postage prices change.

## Why now

No calendar deadline. Postage prices change on a schedule, which makes a "how it works" post more useful than a price post (Claude's judgment). In the pilot it tests the re-check rule on a second official source (USPS).

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** the Forever stamp's meaning, the 1 ounce domestic limit, extra ounces cost more, a pointer to the Global Forever as a different stamp.
- **Out of scope:** retailer questions (Costco and others; no official source), stamp designs and collecting, postcards (not verified), mailing packages, current price history, whether prices will rise again, a how-to-buy guide.
- Global Forever: one short paragraph limited to what the single USPS page says (it is a different stamp). Its price is not given in the article unless a second official source is found and the as-of date is shown. See "Risks".
- Do not recommend actions. Explain what USPS says.

## Image plan (no photos)

Own flow diagram: buy a stamp → postage price rises later → the same stamp still pays the 1 ounce domestic rate → heavier or international mail needs different or additional postage. **No stamp designs, no USPS logo, no photos.** Stamp artwork may be copyrighted.

## Risks

- The USPS international page used for Global Forever is a single source with no page date, and the price list page did not show a Global Forever line. The article keeps it to a pointer.
- Prices (82 cents for a stamp as read on 2026-10-06) change. They are not in the article text.
- Search results probably answer "what is a Forever stamp" directly (Claude's judgment, not checked), so the value has to come from the "what it does not cover" part.
