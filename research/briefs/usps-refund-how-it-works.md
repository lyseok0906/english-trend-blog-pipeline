## Reader problem

A customer paid for postage, a label or an extra service and the service was not performed, or the label was never used. Which of these USPS says it refunds, when to apply, what to bring, how to apply, how the refund is paid, what happens if it is denied, and how a refund differs from an insurance claim.

## User instructions for this post (2026-11-18)

1. Separate the Click-N-Ship 30 days and 60 days: which application route and condition each belongs to, from the USPS.com page and DMM 604.9.
2. Keep the refund (money back for postage or fees) and the claim (compensation for loss or damage of insured mail) apart so readers do not confuse them.
3. Report any conflict with the refund explanations in the published posts.

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** refund against claim; windows by service (DMM chart, USPS.com); Click-N-Ship 30 and 60 days against the DMM PC Postage 30 and 60 days; what to bring; evidence rules; what is not refundable; Priority Mail Express exceptions; how refunds are paid; appeals; differences between the sources.
- **Out:** every dollar amount and percentage, fees, international refunds, business bulk refunds, meters and PC Postage detail, EDDM Retail, PFS-Residential, Postal Store returns, value-added refunds.

## Risks (handled by showing, not resolving)
