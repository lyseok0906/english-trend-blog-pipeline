## Topic

What USPS says is closed and what is delivered on Thanksgiving, as shown in its holiday announcements.

## Working title (proposal)

Is the Post Office Open on Thanksgiving? What USPS Says

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `is the post office open on thanksgiving`
- **Intent:** practical, holiday-timed.
- **Demand signal:** not checked by autocomplete (tool unreliable on 2026-10-07). **Search volume and competition were not checked.**

## Audience

US readers planning mail or errands around Thanksgiving week.

## Angle

1. **The 2025 example (USPS release of Nov. 13, 2025).** USPS observed Thanksgiving on Thursday, Nov. 27: "All Post Office locations will be closed and only Priority Mail Express will be delivered that day. Regular mail delivery and retail services will resume on Friday, Nov. 28."
2. **What still works when Post Offices are closed (USPS).** Self-service kiosks in select lobbies (postage printing for Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and international shipping, stamps, package weighing and mailing, tracking); the 2026 Independence Day release adds that usps.com can be used for stamps, Hold Mail, Change of Address, PO Box rental and Click-N-Ship.
3. **USPS's date can differ from the federal "observed" date (USPS, July 2026).** USPS observed Independence Day on Saturday, July 4, 2026 and Post Offices were open on Friday, July 3; OPM's table shows July 3. This is the reason to say "USPS's announcement" rather than "the federal holiday".
4. **For 2026.** Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 (OPM). The article states USPS's 2026 plan only after USPS announces it.

## Why now

Thanksgiving is 50 days away. See "Timing".

## Timing

Write after USPS's 2026 Thanksgiving release is published (the 2025 release was dated November 13, so mid-November) and before November 26. If the 2026 release is not out by about November 20, either hold the article or publish with the 2025 release clearly dated as an example and say that USPS had not yet announced 2026 plans; the user decides.

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** USPS's statements on closed Post Offices, Priority Mail Express delivery, resumption and the self-service options; the July 2026 contrast; attribution to the named USPS releases with dates.
- **Out:** other holidays (Christmas and New Year's Day are separate items), UPS, FedEx and other carriers, holiday shipping deadlines or pricing, package-delivery guarantees, local Post Office hours, advice on when to mail, state or local rules.
