## Risks

- The guide uses different waits ("1 full postal business day" for most; "24 hours" for No Access) and never defines either. Shown in a table, not reconciled.
- Notice Left says "no automatic second delivery attempt" while No Access says USPS "will automatically reattempt". Shown side by side.
- "Out for Delivery ... will be delivered by a carrier today" can read as a promise; the article pairs it with S2's "USPS Tracking does not expedite or guarantee delivery" and the Expected Delivery Window "not guaranteed".
- The guide's "time limit" for claiming a package has no number on the page; the article says so and does not invent one.
- Neither page has a status definition for every scan a person may see; the article says it follows the 27 listed.
