## Topic

What the TSA 3-1-1 liquids rule says, what it exempts, and what TSA says to do at the checkpoint.

## Working title (proposal)

What Is the TSA 3-1-1 Liquids Rule? What Counts, What's Exempt, What to Expect

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `what is the tsa liquids rule`
- **Intent:** informational. Some readers also ask whether the rule has changed ("new", "current", "now" appear in suggestions).
- **Demand signal:** Google autocomplete (US setting), fetched 2026-10-06, gave 10 suggestions, none naming another country (full list in the source notes). Includes "...for domestic flights", "...for carry ons", "...now", "what is the new tsa liquid rule". **Search volume and competition were not checked.** Commercial travel sites appear to cover this (titles seen in a search; not read).

## Audience

US air travelers and people flying to or through the US who want the rule in plain words.

## Angle

1. **The rule in one picture.** Containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less, in one quart-sized bag.
2. **Where the rule does not apply.** Breast milk, formula and juice over 3.4 oz are exempt from the bag rule and are removed from the carry-on to be screened separately (TSA FAQ). Duty-free liquids bought internationally on a connecting flight to the US have their own conditions (TSA Liquids Rule page). Containers over 3.4 oz that are not exempt go in checked bags (TSA Liquids Rule page).
3. **What the rule does not tell you.** The TSA page gives no update date and does not announce a change. The article says what the TSA page states "as of" the check date and does not say the rule is unchanged unless TSA says so.

## Why now

No calendar deadline. The pilot needs a rule-type post early so the re-check rule can be tested (`docs/blog-c-site-structure.md`). Search interest in whether the rule changed makes an accurate, dated statement useful (Claude's judgment).

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** the 3-1-1 rule as stated on TSA pages, the exemptions listed in section "Angle", checked bags.
- **Out of scope:** medications and other medical liquids (health-adjacent; excluded), specific airports, other countries' liquid rules, TSA scanning technology changes, TSA PreCheck, shoes and devices.
- Do not recommend actions beyond what TSA states. Explain what TSA says.
