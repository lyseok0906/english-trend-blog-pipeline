## Topic

What USPS Hold Mail service does, how long a hold can last, how a request is made and how held mail comes back.

## Working title (proposal)

How Does USPS Hold Mail Work? Limits and Steps

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `how does usps hold mail work`
- **Intent:** informational and practical (before a trip).
- **Demand signal:** not checked by autocomplete (tool unreliable on 2026-10-07). **Search volume and competition were not checked.**

## Audience

US households planning to be away who want to know the limits and the steps.

## Angle

1. **What it is (USPS).** A service that holds mail at the local Post Office until the customer returns, for a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days. USPS's FAQ calls it free (see "Official source notes").
2. **What it holds.** The FAQ says it "pauses ALL mail delivery (letters and packages)" for all individuals at the address, and cannot be used to hold a single package or specific mail items.
3. **How to request.** Online with a USPS.com account and identity verification (USPS Hold Mail page); up to 30 days in advance or as early as the next scheduled delivery day; the FAQ gives the cutoff as 2:00 a.m. Central Time for a same-business-day start. In person with PS Form 8076 is the other route; online submission is not available at all addresses.
4. **When it ends.** On the last day the carrier delivers the held mail, or the customer can pick it up at the Post Office; the FAQ says picked-up mail "must be picked up within 10 days or it will be returned to the senders" and that a carrier delivers only what fits in the receptacle.
5. **Longer than 30 days.** USPS points to a forwarding service; at least 3 days must pass between one hold period and the next.

Not time-bound; some seasonal interest before holiday travel (Claude's judgment).

- **Out:** mail theft or home-safety advice, package holds at pickup (USPS points to a different service; not read), Informed Delivery beyond the one verification link, PO box, business or military specifics, tracking of held mail, whether to use the service.

## Official source notes (read 2026-10-07 in the built-in browser)

- **USPS, "Hold Mail"**, https://www.usps.com/manage/hold-mail.htm . "USPS Hold Mail® service can hold your mail safely at your local Post Office™ facility until you return, for a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days. To hold your mail longer or to reroute your mail, please sign up for a forwarding service." "You can make your request up to 30 days in advance or as early as the next scheduled delivery day. Request your Hold Mail service start date by 3 AM ET (2 AM CT or 12 AM PT) on your requested day, Monday–Saturday." "the USPS Hold Mail process requires you to create or sign in to your USPS.com account and verify your identity before you submit an online request". The meta description says the service "is free". No update date shown.
- **USPS FAQ, "USPS Hold Mail® - The Basics"**, https://faq.usps.com/s/article/USPS-Hold-Mail-The-Basics . Page date shown: Sep 16, 2026 (Article Number 000005321). "USPS Hold Mail® Service is a free service that pauses ALL mail delivery (letters and packages) to an address for a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days". "USPS Hold Mail service cannot be used to hold a single package or specific mail items". "Online USPS Hold Mail requests submitted before 2:00 A.M. Central Time can begin on the same Postal business day." "at least 3 days must elapse between the end of one USPS Hold Mail period and the start of the next one". "Mail must be picked up within 10 days or it will be returned to the senders." "Online submission of USPS Hold Mail service is not available at all addresses."
- **USPS, "Standard Forward Mail"**, https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm : "If you'll be away for 3–30 days, you can "pause" all mail delivery to your address with USPS Hold Mail® service." No update date shown.
