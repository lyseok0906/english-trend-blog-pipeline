## Topic

The date of Thanksgiving in the U.S. in 2026 and how the date is set.

## Working title (proposal)

When Is Thanksgiving 2026 in the U.S.? How the Date Is Set

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `when is thanksgiving 2026`
- **Intent:** informational, a date question; some readers want to know why it moves.
- **Demand signal:** autocomplete on 2026-10-06 (planning note) gave 9 variants such as "in usa", "what day", "how many days", "break", with a Canadian variant. ## Audience

US readers planning travel, work or school around the holiday.

## Angle

1. **The answer.** Thursday, November 26, 2026 (OPM 2026 holiday schedule).
2. **The rule.** The law (5 U.S.C. 6103(a)) says "Thanksgiving Day, the fourth Thursday in November." Since the Thursdays in November 2026 fall on the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th, the fourth is the 26th (calendar computation).
3. **Why the date moves.** A fourth Thursday can fall anywhere from November 22 to 28 (computed over 2000–2099). OPM's tables show November 27 in 2025 and November 25 in 2027.
4. **What "federal holiday" means.** OPM says the federal law establishes the listed public holidays "for Federal employees"; the article does not claim what other employers, schools or states do.

## Why now

Thanksgiving is in 50 days. Publish by roughly mid-November at the latest; there is no search-timing data (Claude's judgment). The article does not depend on any change before then.

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** the date, the statute's wording, the computation, the OPM neighbor-year dates, the federal-employee scope.
- **Out:** the history of the holiday, traditions, travel advice, store or school closings, Canada, the day after Thanksgiving (not a federal holiday on the OPM list; not covered).
- Cross-links: the USPS and TSA Thanksgiving articles (separate briefs, deferred to mid-November and outside the 12-post pilot) are internal-link candidates once published.
