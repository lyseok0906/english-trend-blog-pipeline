## Reader problem

A carrier left a PS Form 3849. How do I get the item delivered again, by when, who can take it, what happens if I miss the return date, and why can I not schedule one?

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** what the notice is; five ways to schedule; cutoffs and dates; change and cancel; one request; who can accept; accountable against non-accountable mail; return dates by mail type; ordinary parcel and Priority Mail Express handling; eligibility and errors; where the pages differ.
- **Out:** prices and fees; Smart Locker detail; international and customs items; perishable parcels; Delivery Instructions detail; misdelivered redelivery notices (second candidate).

## Risks (handled by showing, not resolving)

"Up to" the return date against "not on" the return date; COD 10 days (text) against 30 days (table); change deadline "day before" against a 2 AM CST cutoff in an error message; the unexplained "Refused / Delivered / Out for Redelivery" list; the Priority Mail Express "unless signature waiver is not required" wording; the form's "Smart Locker Eligible" label (earlier "Parcel Locker Eligible").
