## Risks (handled by showing, not resolving)

- The Priority Mail page states "does not come with a money-back guarantee"; the Ground Advantage page is silent. The article says "silent", never "no guarantee".
- Insurance wording differs ("most shipments (restrictions apply)" against "$100 insurance is included"); conditions sit in Priority Mail footnotes and the insurance page.
- The Priority Mail "no surcharges ... Saturday" bullet has a footnote about Priority Mail Express; no conclusion drawn from the Saturday part.
- Whether Prepaid Forever Priority Mail Flat Rate packages get included insurance is not stated (tracking is excluded; insurance needs a tracking barcode); the article says so.
- Dollar figures: only $100 (included insurance) and $5,000 (additional coverage) appear, as coverage amounts. No postage or fee amounts.
