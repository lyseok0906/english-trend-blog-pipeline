## Topic

How long USPS standard mail forwarding lasts, what is forwarded, and what happens when it ends.

## Working title (proposal)

How Long Does USPS Mail Forwarding Last?

## Search intent and focus keyword

- **Proposed focus keyword:** `how long does usps forward mail`
- **Intent:** informational, usually from someone who has moved or is about to.
- **Demand signal:** not checked by autocomplete (tool unreliable on 2026-10-07). **Search volume and competition were not checked.**

## Audience

People who are moving or relocating and want the time limits.

## Angle

1. **The answer (USPS).** "Standard mail forwarding lasts 12 months." A permanent change of address forwards First-Class Mail for 12 months, per the FAQ.
2. **Extending it (USPS).** A paid extension of 6, 12 or 18 more months is possible, 18 months being the maximum; it cannot be canceled or refunded (the article gives no prices).
3. **When it ends (USPS).** USPS returns mail to the sender for 6 months with a label showing the new address.
4. **What is forwarded (USPS page).** First-Class Mail and periodicals; Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail and USPS Ground Advantage are forwarded free; Media Mail is forwarded at the customer's cost for shipping; USPS Marketing Mail is not forwarded.
5. **Starting.** Forwarding "may begin within 3 business days" of the request, but USPS says it is best to allow up to 2 weeks. A temporary change of address covers relocating for 15 days up to 1 year.
6. **A limit USPS states.** A change of address only changes the address with the Post Office; the customer must still update government agencies and companies.

## Why now

Not time-bound. Moving season interest is mostly in summer (Claude's judgment, not measured); this is an evergreen piece.

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** the points above, attributed to USPS with the as-of date.
- **Out:** all prices and fees (including the identity-verification fee, which USPS gives as $1.25 on both pages; a numbered fee dates quickly and scam-site comparisons would need more sources), Premium Forwarding Service, business and military moves, mail for the deceased, international moves, identity-verification troubleshooting, advice on what to update.

## Image plan (no photos)

Own timeline: request → forwarding starts (3 business days to 2 weeks) → 12 months of forwarding (extension branch: +6, +12 or +18 months, maximum 18) → mail returned to sender for 6 months with a new-address label.

## Official source notes (read 2026-10-07 in the built-in browser)

- **USPS, "Standard Forward Mail & Change of Address"**, https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm . "Standard mail forwarding lasts 12 months. You can pay to extend mail forwarding for 6, 12, or 18 more months (18 months is the maximum)." "You can't cancel or request a refund for Extended Mail Forwarding." "When your mail forwarding period ends, USPS will return your mail to the sender for 6 months with a label that has your new address." "Although mail forwarding may begin within 3 business days of your submitted request, it's best to allow up to 2 weeks." "If you're only relocating for 15 days up to 1 year ... submit a temporary change of address request." "First-Class™ mail and periodicals (newsletters and magazines) are forwarded for free." "USPS Marketing Mail® is not forwarded." "Your change of address order only changes your mailing address with the Post Office." No update date shown.
- **USPS FAQ, "Mail Forwarding Options"**, https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Mail-Forwarding-Options . Page date shown: Aug 26, 2026 (Article Number 000007192). A permanent change of address provides forwarding of "primarily First-Class Mail® service for 12 months and Periodicals for 60 days, but generally does not provide the forwarding of USPS Marketing Mail® service or Package Services Mail."

## Risks

- **The two USPS pages differ on periodicals** (the page says periodicals are forwarded for free and does not give a different duration; the FAQ says Periodicals for 60 days). The article states the 12-month period for First-Class Mail only and either leaves periodicals out of the duration or attributes each page separately.
- The FAQ describes forwarding of "primarily" First-Class Mail and "generally" not other classes; the article uses USPS's own qualifiers.
- Heavy competition from third-party pages that also quote the 12 months.
