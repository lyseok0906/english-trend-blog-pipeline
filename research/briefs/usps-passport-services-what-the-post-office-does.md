## Risks

- The rules belong to the State Department and were not read. The article attributes everything to USPS pages and says the State Department pages were not checked.
- Age wording differs: USPS.com "16 or under" against the FAQ "under age 16" (with 16 and 17 year olds listed separately). Shown, not resolved.
- Appointments: USPS.com Step 3 says "you'll need to schedule an appointment" while its banner mentions retail counter booking and limited walk-in hours at select locations. Shown, not resolved. The FAQ adds phone booking.
- Renewal rules appear in detail on USPS.com only; the FAQ sends renewals to the State Department. Shown.
- The FAQ's photo requirements credit a 2017 State Department page; the article says to check the current State Department page.
- No guarantees about timing; no fee amounts.
