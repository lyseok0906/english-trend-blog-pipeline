## Risks (handled by showing, not resolving)

- The DMM chart has no separate domestic Ground Advantage row (it lumps "Insured Mail (including Priority Mail under 503.4.2)"); USPS.com and the FAQ list separate rows with the same numbers.
- USPS.com says the original mailing receipt is required; the DMM accepts a photocopy for insured and COD mail; the FAQ says a claim without a receipt can be filed but payment may be limited.
- Online label record: USPS.com lists it as evidence of insurance; the DMM adds required fields and an evidence of mailing (postmarked receipt or acceptance scan).
- Appeal clock: USPS.com counts from receiving the decision; the FAQ from the date of the decision.
- USPS.com's insurance page gives only the 60-day end for a lost item and no opening day.
- Saved claims: FAQ says deleted after 3 days; USPS.com gives no period.
- No definition of when an item counts as lost; no approval rate.
