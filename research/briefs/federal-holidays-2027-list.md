## Audience

## Angle

1. **The list as OPM prints it** for 2027 (11 rows), with weekdays.
2. **The statutory rule behind each date**, from 5 U.S.C. 6103(a) in one phrase each.
3. **The three moved dates** (June 18, July 5, Dec. 24) and the Dec. 31, 2027 date, attributed to OPM's footnote and 2028 tab; the rule explained by link.
4. **What is not a 2027 federal holiday on OPM's list:** Inauguration Day is a separate holiday for D.C.-area employees every fourth year after 1965 (2025, 2029), not 2027 (statute (c) plus a calculation; to be stated carefully or left out).
