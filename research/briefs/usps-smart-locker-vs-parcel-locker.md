## Reader problem

1. **I got a notice or an email about a locker. Which kind is it, and how long do I have?**
2. **What is a USPS "parcel locker"?** The word names different things on different USPS pages.
3. **Can I send a package to, or from, a Smart Locker, and what are the conditions?**

## Scope: in and out

- **In:** the three kinds side by side (Smart Locker, gopost, cluster box parcel locker); how a package reaches a Smart Locker (store checkout, individual sender, redelivery); sender conditions (services, domestic only, recipient email, size); pickup by QR or access code and the clock (5 days in the locker, 10 at the counter, return at 15; Priority Mail Express 5); hours and After Hours Access; returns from a locker; Local XChange (conditions only); gopost registration and hold time in brief; the cluster box key; the PS Form 3849 box label change ("Parcel Locker Eligible" to "USPS Smart Locker Eligible"); where the pages differ; what they do not say.
- **Out:** prices and fees (a flat fee for Local XChange is mentioned once without an amount; "free" is the pages' own word); locker locations and the locator; the USPS.com page for businesses and API integrations (not read); gopost registration steps beyond a summary; Package Pickup; PO Box service details.
