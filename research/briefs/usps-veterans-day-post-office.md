The two holiday-related 2026 releases that exist were opened: "Postal Service Recommends 2026 Holiday Mailing and Shipping Dates" (Sept. 22, 2026) and "Postal Service Prepped To Deliver for 2026 Holiday Season" (Oct. 22, 2026). Neither mentions Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, "closed" or a Post Office schedule.
- USPS "Holidays & events" page (https://about.usps.com/newsroom/events/, no "last updated" date shown): lists "2026 holidays": Wednesday, November 11, Veterans Day; Thursday, November 26, Thanksgiving Day; Friday, December 25, Christmas Day; and the 2027 holidays (Thursday, November 11, 2027, Veterans Day). The page does **not** say that Post Offices are closed or that delivery stops on these days. Its only operating statement is a footnote about Saturday holidays (Post Offices open on Friday, June 18, and Friday, Dec. 24, 2027).
- Employee and Labor Relations Manual 518.1 (https://about.usps.com/manuals/elm/html/elmc5_008.htm): lists "Veterans' Day November 11" among the 11 days "observed as holidays by the U.S. Postal Service". This is an employee-leave rule; it says nothing about Post Office hours or delivery.
- Last year's notice (USPS national release, Oct. 29, 2025, "U.S. Postal Service To Observe Veterans Day Holiday, Nov. 11"): "The U.S. Postal Service will observe Veterans Day on Tuesday, Nov. 11. All Post Office locations will be closed in recognition of the federal holiday. Only Priority Mail Express will be delivered that day, and regular mail delivery and retail services will resume on Wednesday, Nov. 12." Plus the kiosk and usps.com paragraph. **This is the 2025 plan and is not a statement about 2026.**

## When the 2026 notice may appear (estimate)

For November 11, 2026, that range points to a notice between about October 28 and November 5. Today (October 29) is 13 days ahead and nothing is out yet. This is a pattern in past dates, not a prediction.
