## Computed (not from a source)

- November 1, 2026 is a Sunday (calendar computation), so the Thursdays in November 2026 are the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th; the fourth is the 26th.
- The first Thursday of any November is the 1st to the 7th, so the fourth Thursday (three weeks later) is the 22nd to the 28th. Checked by script over 1900 to 2199: the set of dates is exactly 22 to 28.
- The fourth Thursdays computed for 2024 to 2030 (28, 27, 26, 25, 23, 22, 28) equal OPM's dates.
- Thanksgiving is 50 days after 2026-10-07.
