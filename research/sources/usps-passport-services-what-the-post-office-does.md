## P1 — USPS.com, "Passports" (primary, official)

URL https://www.usps.com/international/passports.htm; page title "Passport Appointments, Renewals, & Photo Services | USPS". No update date. An alert about application maintenance is not used.
- Intro: "Thousands of Post Offices™ accept first-time passport applications for the U.S. Department of State. Most of those locations can also take your passport photo, including digital photos for online renewals." "Renewing Passports: You must renew by mail or online if you're eligible; you cannot renew in person". "First-Time Passports, Passports for Minors, and Passport Photos: Schedule your in-person appointment online."
- Banner: "For first-time passport and photo services, please make an appointment using our online scheduler, a Post Office lobby self-service kiosk, or at a Post Office retail counter. We also offer limited Passport walk-in hours at select locations." ("walk-in" appears once on the page; no location named.)
- First-time: "If you've never had a U.S. passport, are 16 or under, or aren't allowed to renew an existing passport by mail, you need to apply for a new passport in person at the Post Office." "Passports for children under 16 have special requirements." Minors: "A minor's passport cannot be 'renewed.' Instead, each time a minor's passport expires, you must submit a new, first-time passport application in person for them."
- Renewal text: "If you are eligible to renew your passport by mail or online, you must do so—you cannot renew in-person. If you need expedited service for your renewal, you must renew by mail." "If you are not eligible to renew by mail, you must apply in person as a first-time passport applicant."
- Renew by Mail (all "Yes"): passport in your possession; undamaged other than normal wear and tear; "Was issued when you were age 16 or older"; "Was issued within the last 15 years"; "Was issued in your current name or you can document your name change". Form DS-82, mailed directly to the State Department.
- Renew Online: valid for 10 years, expiring within 1 year or expired less than 5 years ago; age 25 or older; not changing personal information such as name or sex; not traveling for at least 6 weeks; "mailing address in a U.S. state or territory" (not an Army, Fleet, or Diplomatic Post Office address); passport in hand, not damaged and not reported lost or stolen. "Online Passport Renewals require a digital photo."
- Step 2 first-time: complete Form DS-11 on the State Department website; "DO NOT SIGN YOUR APPLICATION. A Postal employee must witness your signature."; "Hard copy and digital photo services are available at many Post Offices"; photocopies of proof of identity and citizenship. Step 1 list: application forms (unsigned), proof of identity and a photocopy of the front and back, proof of U.S. citizenship and a photocopy, passport fees.
- Step 3: "Post Offices that offer passport services have set hours, and you'll need to schedule an appointment using the online Retail Customer Appointment Scheduler or a Post Office lobby self-service kiosk." Manage Appointments tab to modify or cancel.
- Step 4 (amounts not used): two basic fees, acceptance (Postal Service) and processing (State Department), "paid separately"; USPS accepts "check or money order, payable to 'Postmaster,' debit card, or credit card"; State Department fees: "You may not pay State Department fees with credit or debit cards" (check or money order).
- Mail renewal: "Department of State recommends using a USPS delivery tracking service" (Priority Mail with Delivery Confirmation or Signature Confirmation, or Priority Mail Express).
- Status: "Online Passport Status System"; "You may not be able to get a status update for 2 weeks after you apply or renew."
- Photos: "You can get hard copy photos for Renew by Mail, or a digital photo for Renew Online"; photo-only appointment.
- Expedited (not used in detail): life-or-death emergencies within 72 hours; urgent travel in less than 10 weeks, call the State Department Passport Agency.

## P2 — USPS FAQ, "U.S. Passports - The Basics" (primary, official)

URL https://faq.usps.com/s/article/U-S-Passports-The-Basics. Article 000007488; dated **Nov 5, 2026**.
- Role: "The Postal Service will: accept a customer's FIRST TIME passport application; take authorized passport photos; review the application and forward the application to the U.S. Department of State for processing. The Postal Service does NOT issue or create passports. Customers CANNOT pick up passports from a Post Office™ facility; they are mailed to the customer from the U.S. Department of State. For those wishing to renew a passport, replace a lost or stolen passport, or provide a name change, please visit the Department of State's website".
- "Courier companies, also known as 'passport expeditors,' are private companies, some of which are allowed to submit passport applications on behalf of customers."
- Renew: "For those wishing to renew a passport online or by mail ... please visit the Department of State's website"; recommended services Priority Mail Express and Priority Mail.
- "You must apply in person using Form DS-11 if at least one of the following is true": first U.S. passport; under age 16; previous passport issued under 16; previous passport lost, stolen or damaged; previous passport issued more than 15 years ago; name changed and unable to legally document it. "If none of the above statements apply to you, you may be eligible to Renew by Mail using Form DS-82."
- Minors under 16: apply in person with both parents or guardians; evidence of relationship; both present acceptable identification; both provide consent. 16 and 17: "Must appear in person at the time of application"; a parent or guardian presents photo ID if the minor has none; parental consent may be requested.
- Appointments: Retail Customer Appointment Scheduler every 15 minutes; online, by phone (IVR, up to 6 applicants), or at a Post Office self-service kiosk; "Passport renewals cannot be made using RCAS or on the kiosk"; USPS employees cannot create appointments online for customers. (No "walk-in" on the FAQ.)
- Photos: plain white or off-white background; 2 x 2 inches; in color; within the last 6 months; eyeglasses removed; "Photo Source: U.S. Department of State 2017". A fee applies at many Post Offices (amount not used). Online renewals require a digital photo.
- Forms: "U.S. Passport Application forms are not provided by USPS". IDs: physical photo ID; "We cannot accept digital IDs."
- Payments: "Two separate payments are required for passport services" (application fee to the State Department; acceptance fee to the Postal Service); "Credit and debit cards CANNOT be accepted" for the State Department fee; USPS fee payment methods listed: cash, money orders, personal checks, debit or credit cards.
- "Processing times begin the day the Department of State receives your application at a passport agency or center, not the day you mail your application or apply for a passport at a local acceptance facility."
- "You may receive your newly issued passport and your citizenship documents in two separate mailings"; contact the National Passport Information Center if a second mailing does not arrive within 10 business days of the first.
- Not used: all dollar amounts, the SSN requirement and penalty, mailing envelope types, expedited service commitment.
