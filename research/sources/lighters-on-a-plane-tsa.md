## S2 — TSA, "Lighters (Arc Lighters, Electronic Lighters, E-Lighters)" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/whatcanibring/items/lighters-arc-lighters-electronic-lighters-e-lighters
- Page date shown: "Last Updated on November 4, 2024".
- Verbatim: "Carry On Bags: Yes (Special Instructions)" "Checked Bags: No" "Measures must be taken to prevent unintentional activation of the heating element while on board the aircraft." ## S5 — FAA PackSafe, "Lighters" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/lighters
- Page date shown: "Last updated: Monday, April 13, 2026".
- Verbatim: "Lighters that use liquid fuel, but do not have an absorbent lining, are forbidden." "Absorbed liquid and butane lighters are limited to one lighter per passenger in carry-on or on one's person." "When a carry-on bag is checked at the gate or at planeside, any lighter in the carry-on must be removed from the bag and kept with the passenger in the aircraft cabin." "Lithium battery powered lighters are allowed in carry on only." "Recharging of the devices and/or the batteries on board the aircraft is not permitted." "Torch lighters (a.k.a. Butane lighters, blue flame lighters, jet flame lighters) are not allowed in the cabin or in checked baggage by both the Hazardous Materials Regulations and TSA security rules."

## S7 — 49 CFR 175.10(a)(2), eCFR (primary, federal regulation)
