## S2 — TSA, "TSA PreCheck FAQ" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/faq
- Page date shown: none. The answers sit in collapsed sections; the page text was read from the page content.
- Verbatim, by question:
  - Showing a card: "Can I access the TSA PreCheck lane by showing my Global Entry, NEXUS, or SENTRI card or TSA PreCheck approval notification? No. To receive TSA PreCheck, you must include your Known Traveler Number (your CBP PASSID for Global Entry, NEXUS, or SENTRI members) in the appropriate field of your airline reservation, and the TSA PreCheck indicator must be displayed on the boarding pass to access the lanes."
  - Indicator: "You must have the TSA PreCheck indicator on your boarding pass before you can access the TSA PreCheck screening lane."
  - Membership: "TSA PreCheck memberships last five years."
  - Family: "Yes, children 17 and under are eligible for TSA PreCheck screening if they meet certain criteria. Anyone 18 and older must have their own Known Traveler Number and enter it when booking their airline reservation. Children 12 and under: Can join you in TSA PreCheck lanes. The TSA PreCheck indicator does not have to be on the child's boarding pass ... Children 13-17: Can join you in the TSA PreCheck lane only when the TSA PreCheck indicator appears on the child's boarding pass." Conditions listed: the adult and the child are on the same airline reservation; the adult's boarding pass has the indicator. "Parents accompanying children through airport security via gate passes will always be directed to standard screening as TSA PreCheck benefits do not apply to gate passes."
  - Lane not available: "If a TSA PreCheck lane is not available, please show your TSA PreCheck boarding pass and you may be able to keep 3-1-1 liquids and laptops in your bag, and shoes and light jackets on while accessing the standard screening lane. Eligible passengers should check with the TSA officer on duty."
  - Guarantee: "If I am TSA PreCheck eligible, am I guaranteed expedited screening? No, TSA uses unpredictable security measures, both seen and unseen, throughout the airport. All travelers will be screened, and no individual is guaranteed expedited screening."
  - International: "TSA PreCheck is available when you depart from a U.S. airport to a foreign country" (not used).

## S3 — TSA, "How to use TSA PreCheck benefits" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/benefits
- Page date shown: none.
- Verbatim: "Please note, TSA PreCheck benefits are not automatic. To get the TSA PreCheck indicator to appear on your boarding pass you must: Add your Known Traveler Number (KTN) in the 'KTN' field of all airline reservations, double-checking you enter the digits correctly. Confirm that your name in the airline reservation matches the name used during enrollment. ... Confirm that your date of birth on the airline reservation is correct. Contact your airline to update your reservation if you have an existing airline reservation that does not include your KTN." Also: "If you enrolled in a trusted traveler program that includes TSA PreCheck benefits: Use your PASS ID as your KTN" (listed for "Global Entry, NEXUS, or SENTRI members"; the PASS ID format details are not used).
