## S1 — USPS FAQ, "Redelivery - The Basics", Article 000005320, dated Sep 16, 2026 (primary, official)

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Redelivery-The-Basics . Page text 21,188 characters after normalization (81 of 83 phrase checks matched at once; the other two failed only on list spacing and were confirmed in the text).
- Scheduling is free; online with a tracking number or the barcode number on the back of PS Form 3849 "We ReDeliver for You!"; the form is left when the receptacle was full, no parcel locker was available, the item was too large, a signature or postage/fees were required, or it could not be left in a secure place; gopost/Smart Locker option only at limited ZIP codes; yellow PS Form 3849-SD for Sunday attempts.
- Availability: "many, but not all, areas"; check with name, address, phone and email on the Redelivery Service page.
- Dates: delivered on the requested date, no specific time; the requested date "can be up to the day the item is scheduled for return to the sender" (Return Date on the final notice) but "scheduling must be done with advance notice and NOT on the date the item will be returned"; calendar days; last-day attempt failing means return; signing the notice and leaving it does not meet the accountable-mail requirement; weather: next business day.
- Who: residential redelivery to the addressee or agent (PS Form 3801 Standing Delivery Order, or a letter to the postmaster; Acceptable Identification at the Post Office); business: an individual employee signs; only to the original address; the carrier is not required to verify an agent at the home or business.
- Without the notice: tracking number or barcode number needed; eligible scan events: Available for Redelivery or Pickup, Notice Left, Receptacle Blocked, Receptacle Full / Item Oversized, No Secure Location Available, No Authorized Recipient Available, Reminder to Schedule Redelivery, Available for Pickup (Customer Pickup and Returned to Sender Only).
- How: QR code ("quickest and most convenient"); online (same day by 2 AM CST Monday through Saturday; USPS Tracking shows the option under "Available Actions" only if eligible; change or cancel no later than the day before; confirmation number emailed; "Edit this request"; Activity History; "Carrier Redelivers" to "Customer will pick up"); paper form option 3 for non-accountable mail (at least 2 delivery days; not for Restricted Delivery, Adult Signature, 3849-SD or Smart Locker-eligible items); phone (tracking number; only carrier redelivery, customer pickup, return to sender; no Smart Locker; without a tracking number the representative can only notify the Post Office); in person (pick up no later than the day before, notice and photo ID, cancels the redelivery); someone else may pick up (not Registered Mail or Restricted Delivery); Informed Delivery can manage redeliveries.
- Errors: address does not match, request already exists, returned to sender; list shows "Refused (by recipient)", "Delivered", "Out for Redelivery" after the reasons (not explained; not used as ineligibility claims); exceeded attempts; maximum of one request; Return to Sender cannot be canceled or modified; session expired. Tracking events: Available For Redelivery or Pickup, Prepared for Redelivery, Out for Redelivery.
- Redelivery against Delivery Instructions: redelivery is after the first attempt; Delivery Instructions before.
- Accountable (Adult Signature, insured mail over a stated value, COD, Registered, Certified, Signature Confirmation, Restricted Delivery, domestic Priority Mail Express "unless signature waiver is not required", Return Receipt) and non-accountable (perishable, USPS Tracking items, uninsured packages, insured mail up to the stated value).
- Priority Mail Express: no automatic attempts after the first; second form on the 3rd calendar day; returned if not picked up or scheduled by close of business 5 calendar days after the first attempt. Ordinary parcel: second attempt next working day with no notice on the first attempt (when someone is normally available); notice after the first attempt when nobody is usually available or it is not known; further attempts only at the customer's request. Signature mail: notice (available the following business day), redelivery not automatic, second notice 5 days after the first, held by service (usually 15 days).
- Top of page: "If you scheduled a Redelivery that did not occur, a service request may be sent by email to your local Post Office."

## S2 — USPS FAQ, "What are the Second and Final Notice and Return Dates for Redelivery?", Article 000006169, dated Apr 7, 2026

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/What-are-the-Second-and-Final-Notice-and-Return-Dates-for-Redelivery . 5,032 characters; 24 of 24 phrase checks.
- Final Notice box on the peach PS Form 3849; the carrier does not have the item with them; returned on the date indicated unless a redelivery is arranged or the item is picked up; text: extra-service articles held 15 days except COD 10 days (unless fewer designated, or Hold For Pickup); table: Certified Mail, Insured Mail, Registered Mail, Return Receipt for Merchandise, Signature Confirmation, Adult Signature Required, USPS Tracking: second/final 5 days after receipt of the first notice, 15 days; COD: 5 days, **30 days**; Priority Mail Express: 3 days after receipt of first notice, 5 days after first attempt; Postage Due 5 days, 15 days; international and perishable rows not used. Calendar days; non-Postal Business day goes to the following Postal Business day; pickup until close of business on the return date; accountable mail returned after 15 days (30 for Customs); two notices (first and final) whether or not a redelivery is scheduled; no further notice after a redelivery scheduled after the final notice fails.

## S3 — USPS FAQ, "PS Form 3849 Redelivery Notice", Article 000006180, dated Jun 29, 2026

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/PS-Form-3849-Redelivery-Notice . 7,116 characters; 17 of 17 phrase checks.
- What the form tells you (mailpiece type, why not left, options: carrier redelivery, Post Office pickup, USPS Smart Locker); Sept 2022 version "USPS Smart Locker Eligible" (earlier "Parcel Locker Eligible"); QR code opens the redelivery page; businesses; someone else may pick up (not Registered Mail or Restricted Delivery); Informed Delivery subscribers can use USPS Electronic Signature Online for domestic Priority Mail Express, Signature Confirmation and insured items over a stated value; signing the notice does not meet the requirement; barcode number works in place of a tracking number; 3849-SD Sunday form: available the next business day (excluding holidays) or redelivery by QR code.
