## S1 — USPS.com, "Rent a PO Box" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.usps.com/manage/po-boxes.htm . Title "Rent a PO Box | USPS". No update date shown (footer "Copyright © 2026 USPS").
- Verbatim: "a PO Box is a secure, numbered box located at the Post Office where your mail is kept until you collect it." "a locked mailbox within a Post Office™ facility" "Apply online: Find, reserve, and pay for a box at a Post Office near you. Pick up your keys or combination in person: Take your completed application and 2 forms of ID to the Post Office (where your box is) to get your keys or lock combination." "Most Post Offices have 5 PO Box sizes, depending on availability". "Prices differ depending on your location." "rental terms of 3-, 6-, and 12-months".
- Sizes (page): "XS - Size 1 ... Holds 10-15 letters and 3 rolled magazines. Size: 3" X 5.5"" "S - Size 2 ... Holds 15+ letters, 5 rolled magazines, or 1 Small Priority Mail Flat Rate® Box. Size: 5" X 5.5"" "M - Size 3 ... Allows large envelopes and magazines to be stacked, and 2 Small Priority Mail Flat Rate Boxes. Size: 5.5" X 11"" "L - Size 4 ... Holds Small and Medium Priority Mail Flat Rate Boxes and still has room for 10-15 letters. Size: 11" X 11"" "XL - Size 5 ... Big enough for multiple packages. Size: 12" X 22.5"".
- Packages: "If you get packages that don't fit into your PO Box, you'll either: Get a slip to pick them up from the counter during normal business hours Get your package from a secure parcel locker (including USPS® Smart Lockers at select locations) at your convenience".
- Online steps: "Step 1: Search for Post Office locations near you ... Step 2: Choose a Post Office location and select your desired PO Box size and payment period. Step 3: Complete the online application form, accept the Terms & Conditions, enter your billing and payment information, and review and submit your application to reserve your PO Box. Step 4: Print your completed form and bring it to the Post Office counter where your box is located, along with 2 forms of acceptable U.S. identification (within 30 days of submitting your online application). After the retail associate verifies your identity, you'll get your PO Box keys or lock combination."
- In person: "download "PS Form 1093, Application for Post Office Box Service," ... (note: if you apply for a PO Box online, this form is automatically generated)" "Bring your payment and 2 forms of acceptable U.S. identification to the Post Office".
- Unavailable box: "choose another box size, find a box at a different location, or request to be added to the wait list ... We'll contact you via an email or phone call when the box becomes available. You'll then have 2 days to reserve the box before it goes to the next person on the wait list."
- Other city: "Can I get a PO Box in a different city or state? Yes, you can. However, you are still required to visit the Post Office where your box is located, with the proper documentation, to pick up your PO Box keys or combination."
- Payment and terms: "You have 4 different options for making a PO Box payment: Online with a credit or debit card ... Pay at a self-service kiosk (SSK) ... Pay by mail ... Pay in person". "3 payment term options: 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months. A 3-month payment term requires enrolling in automatic renewals with no opt-out. However, if you choose a 6- or 12-month period, you can opt out of automatic renewals." "You must make payments by the 10th of the month the payment is due. If you don't make this payment, your PO Box will be closed on the 11th of the month." "You pay for a full rental period at the beginning; however, if you decide to close your box, you'll get a partial refund depending on how long you've had the box."
- Keys and access: "you'll get 2 keys or the lock combination for your box. If you need more than 2 keys, you can request more for an additional fee." "Most PO Box locations allow 24/7 access to your PO Box; however, hours vary by Post Office location."
- Street Addressing: "With Street Addressing (if available), you have the option of using the street address of your Post Office location, combined with your PO Box number, as your mailing address. This lets you receive packages from private carriers (such as UPS, FedEx, DHL, and Amazon), as long as they comply with USPS mailing standards."
- Signature: "For deliveries that require a signature, we'll leave a delivery notice inside your PO Box, and you'll go to the counter to sign for and pick up your package. If you sign up for the free Signature on File service, we'll leave the item in your PO Box."
- Closing: "You can close a PO Box by going to Manage PO Box, signing in to your account, and clicking the Close/Request Refund link ... You can also request a PO Box be closed by informing the retail associate at the Post Office where your box is located. Once a box is closed, be sure to submit a Change of Address (COA) form. ... Your mail can be forwarded to your new address for up to one year."

## S2 — Domestic Mail Manual 508, section 4.0 "Post Office Box Service" (primary, official)

- URL: https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/508.htm . No revision date shown on the page.
- Verbatim: "Post Office Box (PO Box) service is a premium service offered for a fee (See Notice 123-Price List) to any customer and for no fee to customers who are not eligible for carrier delivery (also see 4.5.2)." (4.1.1) "There are five box sizes and availability of sizes varies by facility." (4.1.3) with the chart: size 1 under 296 cubic inches, 3 by 5.5; size 2 296 through 499, 5 by 5.5; size 3 500 through 999, 11 by 5.5; size 4 1,000 through 1,999, 11 by 11; size 5 2,000 or more, 22.5 by 12. "Mail intended for delivery through a box must show the assigned PO box number in the address immediately above the city, state, and ZIP Code." (4.1.4) "PO Box service is available in 3-, 6- or two 6-month (semi-annual) prepaid periods. The 3-month option requires establishing an automatic recurring renewal payment ... The 3-month option is not available at Post Office locations on the semi-annual (April/October) payment schedule." (4.1.7)
- Application (4.2.1): "Customers may apply for PO Box service by completing PS Form 1093, Application for Post Office Box Service, at a Post Office or online and presenting it to any postal retail facility. When submitting an application, all applicants listed on the application (including both spouses and other individuals listed, except for minors) each must present two items of valid, current identification. One must be an acceptable primary form of identification, as specified under 608.10.3, and the other an acceptable secondary form of identification, as described under 608.10.4." "Minors (a person under 18 years of age) may be provided with PO Box service, unless the minor's parent or guardian submits a written objection to the appropriate postmaster, but are ineligible to apply for PO Box service online." "Spouses may complete and sign one Form 1093." "The applicant must present an unsigned printout of the online Form 1093 in person to the Post Office and, upon approval, receive the box number and keys or lock combination."
- Availability (4.1.5, 4.1.6): when no box of the right size is available the postmaster may refer the customer elsewhere, use a waiting list, provide general delivery until a box is available, or offer another size or caller service; "Online customers cannot apply for a box that is not available but can place themselves on a waiting list for the box."
- Keys (4.7): "Two PO Box keys are initially issued to each new box customer." refundable key deposit except for new Competitive customers in groups 30-44; additional keys by Form 1094 with a deposit and a non-refundable fee.
- Surrender (4.8.3): "A PO box is surrendered if: A box customer refuses or fails to pay the proper fees by the due date. A box customer submits a written notice to discontinue service. ... A box customer ... submits a permanent change-of-address order".

## Where the two sources differ in wording (stated in the article, not resolved)

- **Terms:** the USPS.com page lists 3, 6 and 12 months; the DMM text read lists 3-, 6- or two 6-month (semi-annual) prepaid periods and does not mention 12 months.
- **Identification:** the page says 2 forms of acceptable U.S. identification; the DMM requires one primary and one secondary form for each listed applicant (spouses and others, except minors) and points to 608.10.3 and 608.10.4, which this work did not read. The article does not list acceptable IDs.
- **Page-only points:** the 30-day window for online applicants, the 2-day wait-list hold, the payment deadline (10th, closed on the 11th), partial refund wording, Street Addressing, Signature on File and the one-year forwarding statement appear on the page only (not looked for in the DMM sections read).
