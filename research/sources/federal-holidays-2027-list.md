## S3 — 5 U.S.C. 6103, govinfo.gov (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title5/html/USCODE-2024-title5-partIII-subpartE-chap61-subchapI-sec6103.htm (page shows "2024 Edition"; the weekend article's note used the 2023 edition).
- (a) verbatim: "The following are legal public holidays: New Year's Day, January 1. Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr., the third Monday in January. Washington's Birthday, the third Monday in February. Memorial Day, the last Monday in May. Juneteenth National Independence Day, June 19. Independence Day, July 4. Labor Day, the first Monday in September. Columbus Day, the second Monday in October. Veterans Day, November 11. Thanksgiving Day, the fourth Thursday in November. Christmas Day, December 25."
- (b)(1) verbatim: "Instead of a holiday that occurs on a Saturday, the Friday immediately before is a legal public holiday for— (A) employees whose basic workweek is Monday through Friday; and (B) the purpose of section 6309 of this title." (the footnote number "1" after 6309 in the page text is omitted). The text of subsection (b) read does not contain a rule moving a Sunday holiday to Monday (the word "Sunday" appears only in (b)(2), about employees whose regular non-workday is scheduled instead of Sunday); OPM cites Executive Order 11582 for the Sunday rule.
- (c): "January 20 of each fourth year after 1965, Inauguration Day, is a legal public holiday" for the listed D.C.-area employees. Not a 2027 date (2025 and 2029).
