## S1 — USPS.com, "Request a USPS Refund: Domestic" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.usps.com/help/refunds.htm . On-page heading "Request a USPS Refund: Domestic"; browser title "Request a Domestic Refund | USPS". No update date shown. Page text 11,306 characters after normalization (50 of 50 phrase checks on 2026-11-18). A first read the same day (11,561 characters) had the same content.
- Intro: "To submit a USPS refund request (including fees or postage), you'll need proof of purchase." May need: tracking number, purchase receipt, photo ID (if in person). "(To file a claim for insured mail that was lost, damaged, or missing contents, see File a USPS Claim: Domestic.)" "You can request refunds for Priority Mail Express items and certain extra services online." Businesses may upload files with up to 1,000 tracking numbers (not used).
- Click-N-Ship labels: "Unused Click-N-Ship labels are eligible for refunds up to 60 days after the print date (the label transaction date)." Eligible: labels not scanned by the postage system; "Labels created within the past 30 days of label print date"; labels with no refund request. Route: Click-N-Ship account, Shipping History, check labels, Refund Labels, Proceed. Older: "If a label was printed more than 30 but less than 60 days ago, email the Click-N-Ship Help Desk" with username, account number, label number, transaction number and date.
- Extra services: only the person who paid; eligible: non-delivery when USPS is at fault, incorrectly charged postage or fees, paid services not provided; "30 to 60 days after the mailing date for general extra services" and "30 to 60 days after the mailing date if the extra service is purchased with Priority Mail Express"; tracking number and mailing receipt. Online: Certified Mail, Return Receipt (paper and electronic), Signature Confirmation, USPS Tracking for USPS Marketing Mail; otherwise take proof documents to the Post Office where you paid.
- Priority Mail Express: money-back guarantee; one refund request per tracking number; combine refunds for the service and related extra services; guaranteed arrival time is on the receipt; "2 to 30 days after the mailing date for Priority Mail Express without extra services", "30 to 60 days" if an extra service was purchased; online with a free USPS.com account, or at a Post Office: Part I of Form 3533 in duplicate with the original customer copy of Label 11.
- PO Boxes: box in use, "Request a pro-rated refund for unused time in your PO Box service agreement" at the Post Office where the box is; new box, online if applied online, unused, keys not picked up, "Request a refund within 30 days of your original payment".
- Package Intercept: the fee "plus any applicable postage charge may be refundable if the shipment was not intercepted" (the page states the fee amount; not used); online; normally processed within 2 to 3 business days unless more information is needed; up to 2 billing cycles to appear on the card. Pickup On Demand: only if the carrier fails to show up during the specified day; not eligible if the carrier shows up or if cancelled after 5 a.m. local time on the pickup day; same processing wording.
- After submitting: online refunds by check by mail; Post Office refunds in cash, check or money order; Click-N-Ship refunds credited to the original payment account; business customers online by check. "If your refund was only partially paid or denied, you may file a dispute within 30 days of receiving the decision."
- **Not on the page:** a row for postage on ordinary mail (no "10 days" anywhere); a processing time for Click-N-Ship, extra-service or Priority Mail Express refunds; the "Change of Address" refund that the FAQ lists.
- Other sections not used: EDDM Retail, Premium Forwarding Service Residential, Postal Store Returns & Exchanges, international refunds (separate page, not read).

## S2 — DMM 604, "Postage Payment Methods and Refunds", section 9, "Exchanges and Refunds" (primary, official)

- URL: https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/604.htm . No page date shown (some paragraphs carry bracketed amendment dates, e.g. [4-6-26], [7-12-26]). Page text 129,313 characters; 34 of 34 phrase checks (two corrected for the wording of my phrase, not the source).
- 9.2: "A customer may apply for a refund of postage and fees as provided under 9.2. For information on filing an indemnity claim for loss or damage see 609." 9.2.1: refunds "if postage and retail or extra service fees are paid and no service is rendered, or if the amount collected was more than the lawful price"; Exhibit 9.2.1, from the mailing date: Priority Mail Express 2 to 30 days; with an extra service 30 to 60; All Other Classes of Mail 10 to 60; with extra service(s) (9.2.4h) 30 to 60.
- 9.2.3 full refund cases (USPS at fault; paid above lawful price; Certified Mail, USPS Tracking or Signature Services fee when the extra service was not received; Priority Mail Express not delivered per the standard except 9.5.5; others, not all used).
- 9.2.4 not available (used): COD, Priority Mail Express insurance, insured mail and Registered Mail fees after acceptance; unused adhesive stamps (exchange under 9.1); no postmarked or retail mailing receipt or valid acceptance/processing scan; undeliverable Certified Mail and Signature Confirmation fees when a scan shows an attempted delivery; extra-service fees when the request is made "less than 30 days or more than 60 days from the date the service was purchased"; Package Intercept per-piece fee once intercepted.
- 9.2.5: online (preferred) for domestic Priority Mail Express and extra services only (Certified Mail, Return Receipt, Signature Confirmation, USPS Tracking for USPS Marketing Mail); evidence retained, may be uploaded as .jpeg, .pdf or .png with the mailing date; appeals of denied online requests online "within 30 days of receipt of the denial". Form 3533 to the postmaster for all refunds, with envelope or wrapper showing names and addresses, canceled postage and markings or other evidence; adverse rulings appealed through the postmaster to the manager (no number of days). Automated: the USPS Connect Local Sunday fee refund through Click-N-Ship (the only mention of Click-N-Ship on the page).
- 9.2.6: online Priority Mail Express refunds by check or account credit; extra services by check; Form 3533 immediate refund in cash or a no-fee money order (up to a dollar limit, not used) or deferred refund by check or account credit.
- 9.3 (postage evidencing systems, not Click-N-Ship by name): meter indicia requests within 60 days of the date in the indicia (9.3.2); PC Postage with a package identification code: started within 30 days of printing, electronically to the provider (9.3.10); without one: physically to the provider within 60 days of the date in the indicia (9.3.11). Percentage limits and fees in 9.3 are not used.
- 9.5 (Priority Mail Express): 9.5.1 refund for an item not delivered, not attempted, or not made available by the stated date and time; 9.5.3 only the mailer may apply; 9.5.5 no refund for strike or work stoppage, undeliverable as addressed, held at the addressee's written request, a 1-Day shipment mailed December 22 through 25 delivered or attempted within 2 business days, and non-loss refunds to or from Alaska, Hawaii, Guam, American Samoa, the Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, the Marshall Islands and Micronesia (Puerto Rico is not listed); 9.5.6 apply online or Form 3533 with Label 11 or the mailing receipt.
- 9.6: PO Box fee refunds are provided under 508.4.6 (not read).
