## S1 — USPS FAQ, "Where is my package? Tracking Status Help" (primary, official)

URL https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Where-is-my-package. Article 000006077; dated **Sep 29, 2026**. Five status groups (headings as shown): DELIVERED / DELIVERY ATTEMPTED (8 statuses), OUT FOR DELIVERY (5), MOVING THROUGH NETWORK (5), PREPARING FOR DELIVERY (1), ACCEPTANCE / PRESHIPMENT (8): 27 listed statuses. Each entry has "What It Means" and "What You Can Do".
- Delivered: "delivered with a recorded date, time and delivery location"; check garage, household, mailbox ("check again later in the day"), porch, areas protected from weather, areas out of street view ("under a mat or near a back entrance"), exterior doors; multi-unit community mailbox with a key in the regular compartment: "your package is in a parcel locker. Check the key tag for the corresponding locker number"; apartment or condo: contact the building's management office; "more than one postal business day since the 'delivered' scan ... visit the email us page on usps.com to submit a service request to your local Post Office for eligible items."
- In Transit / Arrived at USPS Facility / Departed USPS Facility: "currently being processed or transported to your delivering Post Office"; "may appear multiple times from the same facility and/or on different days as it is traveling".
- Out for Delivery: "prepared for delivery at your local Post Office and will be delivered by a carrier today. Not all packages are delivered by the same carrier who delivers your regular mail and may arrive at a different time or later in the day." Action: "If the package hasn't been delivered after 1 full postal business day has passed following this scan, use your tracking number and submit a service request to your local Post Office."
- Shipping partner statuses: "USPS does not have possession of the package"; contact the shipper.
- Insufficient Address / No Such Number: returned to sender; "The sender will need to reship the item with new postage".
- No Access to Delivery Location: carrier could not get to the mailbox, porch or garage; reasons (vehicle blocking, loose dog, gated community or locked building, public utility work, emergency responders); "We will automatically reattempt delivery the next business day"; "If it's been more than 24 hours since the No Access status, use your tracking number and submit a service request".
- Notice Left (No Authorized Recipient Available): signature needed; PS Form 3849 Redelivery Notice; "There will be no automatic second delivery attempt." Three options: schedule a redelivery (tracking number or the barcode number on the notice; Smart Locker if indicated on the notice); pick up at the listed Post Office (authorized agent allowed; valid photo ID); request return to sender with the Redelivery application. "If you don't claim the package within the time limit, we will return it to the sender." (no number given)
- Notice Left (No Secure Location Available): "The carrier did not see a secure place to leave the package and returned it to the Post Office. There will be no automatic second delivery attempt"; the same three options.
- Available for Pickup: "The package cannot be delivered ... A redelivery cannot be scheduled for this package"; pickup "beginning the next business day" or return to sender; photo ID.
- Shipping Label Created / Pre-Shipment, USPS Awaiting Item: "The package has not yet been given to USPS"; contact the shipper.
- Arrived at Post Office: "should arrive today or the next postal business day"; "no change in tracking status after 1 full postal business day has passed following the arrival date ... AND no delivery has occurred ... submit a service request".
- USPS in Possession of Item: "No action is needed at this time".
- Forwarded / Forward Processed: "due to a change of address request or because the package is at the wrong Post Office for delivery".
- Redelivery Scheduled / Reminder to Schedule Redelivery: the same three options.
- Delivery Status Not Updated / Awaiting Delivery Scan: "A Delivered status was not recorded by 9pm local time on the date of the Out for Delivery status and we now anticipate delivery of your package on the next business day"; "If more than 1 full postal business day has passed since this status, AND no delivery has occurred ... submit a service request".
- Held at Post Office, At Customer Request: due to "a USPS Hold Mail request, Hold for Pickup request or USPS Delivery Instructions request. A redelivery cannot be scheduled for this package"; valid Hold Mail requests are handled "according to the instructions in the request (carrier delivery or customer pickup)"; otherwise "you must pick up your package ... You must show valid photo ID".
- Shipment Received, Package Acceptance Pending: a container or pallet received; processing not started.
Absence tests (article body): no "$", no "fee", no "price", no "guarantee"; "1 full postal business day" is not defined; "time limit" appears 5 times and none of its contexts has a number; "eligible items" is not defined.

## S2 — USPS FAQ, "USPS Tracking® - The Basics" (primary, official)

URL https://faq.usps.com/s/article/USPS-Tracking-The-Basics. Article 000007991; dated **Sep 17, 2026**.
"USPS Tracking does not expedite or guarantee delivery." Tracking starts "as soon as the item's sender or our shipping partner notifies us electronically that we should expect a package", continues with acceptance and "ends when the item is delivered or picked up by the recipient or the recipient's agent". "Scanning information is updated throughout the day as scans become available." Status meanings: "check the Scan Event Status page".
First-Class Mail "postcards, letters and flats are not eligible for USPS Tracking service"; letters and flats can get extra services such as Certified Mail and Registered Mail that require barcodes.
Delivery standard: look up the mail class under "Product Information" and compare to the Mail Delivery Standards chart in "Delayed mail and packages?"; status "Alert": check USPS Service Alerts first.
Expected Delivery Window: "a 2-hour slot of time when you can expect your package to arrive. Delivery within that time is not guaranteed".
Ways to track: online, text 28777 (2USPS), USPS Mobile app, phone. Notifications: "Email and text update requests are active for a period of 2 weeks following the request"; Informed Delivery Daily Digest as another option; reply STOP to 28777 ends all active text tracking requests.
Records: USPS Tracking 120 days; Signature Confirmation 1 year; Priority Mail Express, Certified Mail, Registered Mail, Numbered International Mail Services and Adult Signature services 2 years.
"The absence of a delivery scan on a mailpiece does not necessarily indicate that the item was not delivered. It is possible the piece was delivered but the scan was not captured. If you think this has happened, you may wish to contact the sender or recipient to confirm delivery of the item." (The next sentence is about a tracking-fee refund for Marketing Mail parcels: not used.)
Not used: any fee or cost statement.

## Differences shown in the article (not resolved)

1. Waits: "1 full postal business day" (Out for Delivery, Arrived at Post Office, Not Updated, Delivered) against "more than 24 hours" (No Access); neither is defined.
2. After a delivery problem: No Access is reattempted automatically; the two Notice Left statuses have "no automatic second delivery attempt"; Available for Pickup and Held at Post Office: "A redelivery cannot be scheduled".
3. "Will be delivered by a carrier today" (Out for Delivery) against S2's "does not expedite or guarantee delivery" and the Expected Delivery Window "not guaranteed".
