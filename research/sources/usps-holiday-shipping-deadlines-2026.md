## S1 — USPS.com, "2026 Holiday Shipping Deadlines" (primary, official)

URL https://www.usps.com/holiday/holiday-shipping-dates.htm (no update date; an ALERT banner about application maintenance on Nov. 12 between 9 and 10 PM ET is not used).
Verbatim (selected): "Cutoff Dates for December 25. If you're shipping holiday cards and gifts for Christmas (Dec. 25), Kwanzaa (Dec. 26 - Jan. 1), or other holiday traditions, find the USPS recommended domestic, international, and military deadlines in these tables. Please note that Hanukkah (Dec. 4 - Dec. 12) is earlier this year so please adjust accordingly." Also: "Get free shipping supplies online or at your local Post Office location. Print shipping labels. Schedule a free package pickup."
Contiguous U.S., "Recommended send-by dates for expected delivery before December 25": USPS Ground Advantage Service Thursday, Dec. 17; First-Class Mail Service Thursday, Dec. 17; Priority Mail Service Friday, Dec. 18; Priority Mail Express Service Saturday, Dec. 19.
Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and U.S. Territories: Ground Advantage "See below"; First-Class Mail Thursday, Dec. 17; Priority Mail Friday, Dec. 18; Priority Mail Express Saturday, Dec. 19.
Footnotes: (1) "Actual delivery date may vary and is based on origin, destination, Post Office acceptance time, and other conditions." (2) CBP "requires that mailers prepay duties, taxes, and fees, and obtain proof of payment on all articles valued up to $2,500 entering the customs territory of the United States (CTUS) from the following insular possessions and territories: American Samoa, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, and U.S. Virgin Islands. Please review the Mailing Standards of the United States Postal Service, Domestic Mail (DMM) for details." (3) "Ground Advantage packages that are shipped to or from offshore ZIP codes (AK, HI, PR, and U.S. Territories) may experience longer transit times and extended Estimated Delivery Dates (EDD). Please review the EDD for these ZIP codes and adjust the induction date as needed." (4) "USPS Ground Advantage packages between the contiguous United States and the following locations will have a 10-day or more service expectation: Alaska, Hawaii, American Samoa, Puerto Rico, United States Virgin Islands, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands and the Freely Associated States. Days will vary due to the range of distances between locations. Customers can look up service expectations on usps.com for mailings from one ZIP Code to another on a particular mailing date: https://postcalc.usps.com/ServiceCommitments." (5) "As of August 1, 2023, USPS will no longer process refund requests for guaranteed service on these items. Refund requests for loss will still be processed."
International (each of Africa; Asia/Pacific Rim; Australia/New Zealand; Canada; Caribbean; Central & South America; Europe; Mexico; Middle East): First-Class Package International Service Wednesday, Dec. 9; Priority Mail International Service Wednesday, Dec. 9; Priority Mail Express International Service Wednesday, Dec. 16.
Military (APO/FPO/DPO AE ZIPs 090-092; 093; 094-099; 340; 962-966): Ground Advantage Wednesday, Nov. 4; First-Class Mail Wednesday, Dec. 9; Priority Mail Wednesday, Dec. 9; Priority Mail Express Military Wednesday, Dec. 16 (ZIP 093: N/A). "PMEMS is available to selected military/diplomatic Post Offices. Check with your local Post Office to determine if this service is available to an APO/FPO/DPO addresses."
