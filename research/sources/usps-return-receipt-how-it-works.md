## R1 — USPS FAQ, "Return Receipt - The Basics", Article 000006076, dated Sep 18, 2026 (primary, official)
- https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Return-Receipt-The-Basics . 45 phrase checks, 44 at once (the page text runs two words together in one sentence, so one phrase differed; the sentence was read).
- Proof of delivery contents; purchase at the time of mailing only; request for delivery information if it did not arrive; mail or email; link to USPS Tracking; mailing from home, office or collection box; the Related Tracking Number and that a Return Receipt cannot be viewed online; classes and extra services (some marked "Form 3811 only"); the international pink card PS Form 2865; 90-day request with PS Form 3811-A; refund conditions and the 30 to 60 day wording; the 30 calendar days or after delivery wording for retail purchases; the 3 to 5 day advice for electronic receipts. ## R2 — USPS FAQ, "Electronic Return Receipt", Article 000007257, dated May 18, 2026
- https://faq.usps.com/s/article/What-is-Electronic-Return-Receipt . 29 of 29 phrase checks.
- Equivalent to the hard copy; legal status left to courts; records kept two years, slower after 60 days; not purchasable on USPS.com, bought at Post Offices; not for APO, FPO, DPO, territories, possessions, freely associated states; request at mailing and then by USPS Tracking with the article number and an email address; immediate if delivered, otherwise usually within 48 hours of delivery; the email address is entered on the website; Bulk Proof of Delivery (25 items per day); signature extract file.

- https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/503.htm . 13 of 13 phrase checks within section 6.0.
