## S1 — USPS FAQ, "Package Pickup & Pickup on Demand" (primary, official)

URL https://faq.usps.com/articles/FAQ/What-is-Package-Pickup (title "Knowledge: Package Pickup & Pickup on Demand®", Article Number 000006808; no update date on the page; the words "Media Mail" and "Library Mail" do not appear).
Verbatim (selected): "The Postal Service has three different options for pickup of outgoing mail. Pickup on Demand® is perfect for when you need to have parcels picked up on a specific date in a specific timeframe (within a 2-hour window). A pickup fee is charged per trip, regardless of the number of items scheduled for pickup. Package Pickup allows you to schedule a free pickup for the next day or a designated day at the time the letter carrier delivers your mail. Collection Service refers to prepaid mail deposited in a collection box or prepaid mail customers hand directly to a letter carrier or other postal employee." "You can schedule, edit or cancel a Pickup on Demand Monday - Saturday until 5:00 AM local time on the day of the pickup. You can schedule, edit or cancel a Package Pickup Monday - Saturday until 2:00 AM Central Time on the day of the pickup. Package Pickup can occur at any time on the scheduled date." "Individual items cannot exceed 70 pounds or 130 inches in total length and girth." "Please note: Not all addresses are eligible for Package Pickup." "Your letter carrier can only pick up packages with prepaid postage and/or return merchandise that are eligible for the free Package Pickup or the paid Pickup on Demand service." Package Pickup list: Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, USPS Ground Advantage, Returns, International Priority Mail Express, International Priority Mail, First-Class Package International Service; "Packages of other mail classes, such as First-Class Mail® items, may also be picked up if they are combined with one of the aforementioned premium products." Pickup on Demand list adds USPS Connect Local, Package Service parcels, Priority Mail Express and Priority Mail Open and Distribute Shipments. Priority Mail Express: "If you want the Priority Mail Express guarantee for a specific mailing, the item(s) must be presented to an acceptance employee at the Post Office location. You will need to contact your local Post Office for the guarantee details in your area". International: "International packages can only be picked up if the shipping label, customs forms and postage payment were completed online. Packages with hand-written customs forms must be taken to a Post Office retail location for mailing." Limitations: hazardous materials must meet DMM or IMM standards, "This includes items that contain anything fragile without proper packaging or liquid items."; "Size limitations: 70 pounds 130 inches in total length and girth"; "Mailpieces weighing more than 10 oz. or more than 1/2-inch in thickness bearing postage stamps for postage must be presented by the customer to an employee at a retail counter at a Post Office". Postage: "Sufficient postage must be properly applied to your mailpiece(s) before the pickup. You can purchase postage online using the Click-N-Ship®, PC Postage®, or Postage Meters."; prepay list: stamps (item 10 oz or less and 1/2-inch in thickness or less), postage meter imprint, PC Postage products, online Click-N-Ship labels with postage, prepaid Priority Mail Flat Rate envelope, return items. "The letter carrier is not required to verify postage; however, offices will engage in standard revenue protection practices if packages appear underpaid." Scheduling: request on a holiday or weekend accepted, pickup on the next scheduled delivery day (Saturday afternoon request for Monday unless Monday is a Postal holiday); up to 6 months in advance; login needed for future and repeat requests; any number of days during the next six months; package locations (In/At Mailbox, On the Porch, Front Door, Back Door, Side Door, Knock on Door/Ring Bell, Mail Room, Office, Reception, Other); multi-story buildings: ground level, secured location; carrier discretion; "Hazards such as snow, ice, obstructed pathways and dogs may prevent the letter carrier from collecting packages."; confirmation email or notice, and if not collected "the notice will detail why"; modify or cancel "before 2:00 am and Pickup on Demand before 5:00 am local time the day of the scheduled pickup" via "Modify an Existing Pickup" (date, location, number of packages, total weight); same-day volume or weight increase: call the local Post Office; delivery status for Priority Mail Express or packages with USPS Tracking or Signature Confirmation; "How do I find the fees for Pickup on Demand? Fees are listed in Notice 123, under Domestic - Other Services and Fees."; "Do I need to be present for pickup? No, as long as you are shipping postage-paid items within the United States that have been properly prepared for pickup and left in a safe location for the letter carrier."; problems: contact the local Post Office and file a service request at Email Us > Where is my Mail?; Pickup on Demand refunds can be requested online if the service was not performed according to its service features.

## S2 — USPS.com, "How to Prepare & Send a Package" (primary, official)

URL https://www.usps.com/ship/packages.htm (no update date). Step 6: "If your package is less than one-half inch thick and weighs less than 10oz, you can use postage stamps and drop off your package by: Putting it in your mailbox for carrier pickup. Dropping it in a blue collection box or Post Office lobby drop-off box. Requesting a free pickup. If your package uses stamps and is more than one-half inch thick or weighs more than 10 oz, you must take it to a Post Office counter and give it to the Retail Associate at the counter. If you printed the postage using Click-N-Ship or at a USPS kiosk, you can use any method to drop off your packages." Note: "If your stamped package is thicker than one-half inch or heavier than 10 oz-if you put it in your mailbox for pickup, the carrier will leave it. If you drop it in a blue collection box or Post Office lobby mail receptacle, it will be returned to you." "Schedule a Package Pickup ... Pick up during regular mail delivery (free!): USPS will pick up eligible packages for free. Pickup On Demand (premium paid service): For a fee, you can select a 2-ho[ur window]".
