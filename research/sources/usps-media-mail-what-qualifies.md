## S1 — USPS.com, "Mail & Shipping Services", Media Mail section (primary, official)

URL https://www.usps.com/ship/mail-shipping-services.htm (title "Mail & Shipping Services | USPS"; no update date; "Copyright © 2026 USPS"). The old URL https://www.usps.com/ship/media-mail.htm shows "This Page Has Moved" and links to this page.
Verbatim (prices removed): "Media Mail Shipping in 2–8 Days6 Media Mail® is a cost-effective way to send eligible books, media, and educational materials. You can only send certain types of media: If you try to include anything that's not allowed, you (or your recipient) will have to pay higher postage rates. All Media Mail packages are subject to opening and inspection by the Postal Service." "Using Media Mail Here's what you can send: Books (at least 8 pages) 16-millimeter or narrower width films Printed music and test materials Video and sound recordings (including tapes, albums, and CDs/DVDs) Playscripts and manuscripts Printed educational reference charts Medical loose-leaf pages and binders Computer-readable media NOTE: Video games, computer drives, and digital drives do not qualify for Media Mail prices." "Weight, Size, & Shape Requirements Maximum weight is 70 lbs. See the full list of requirements and allowed items: Media Mail Eligibility Standards (Note: Library Mail is a related service for eligible organizations, including schools, museums, nonprofits, and other groups.)" (the "Media Mail Eligibility Standards" link goes to https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/173.htm#ep1113500). "Additional Package Fees Double-check your package measurements and contents: You'll pay additional fees if you're shipping a package that is large and lightweight, has a nonstandard size, shape, or material, or has perishable items." Footnote 6: "The timeliness of service to or from destinations outside the contiguous U.S. may be affected by the limited availability of transportation."

## S2 — Domestic Mail Manual 173, "Media Mail and Library Mail" (primary, official)

URL https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/173.htm (Postal Explorer; title "170 Retail Mail Media Mail and Library Mail"; no revision date on the page).
Verbatim (selected): 1.2 "Media Mail and Library Mail pieces are charged per pound or fraction thereof; any fraction of a pound is considered a whole pound." 2.1 "Media Mail and Library Mail are subclasses of Package Services. The USPS does not guarantee the delivery of Media Mail or Library Mail within a specified time." 2.2 "Media Mail and Library Mail are not sealed against postal inspection. Regardless of physical closure, the mailing of articles at Media Mail or Library Mail prices constitutes consent by the mailer to postal inspection of the contents." 2.3 "All Media Mail and Library Mail must bear a delivery address. The delivery address on each piece must include the correct ZIP Code or ZIP+4 code. Each piece must bear the sender's return address." 3.0 "Mailable matter meeting one of the specific eligibility standards in 4.0 for Media Mail and 5.0 for Library Mail, and that is neither mailed or required to be mailed as First-Class Mail nor entered as Periodicals, may be mailed as Media Mail or Library Mail, respectively." 4.1 "Only these items may be mailed at the Media Mail prices:" Books "of at least eight printed pages, consisting wholly of reading matter or scholarly bibliography, or reading matter with incidental blank spaces for notations and containing no advertising matter other than incidental announcements of books" (advertising includes paid advertising and the publishers' own advertising in display, classified or editorial style); "16-millimeter or narrower width films, which must be positive prints in final form for viewing, and catalogs of such films of 24 pages or more (at least 22 of which are printed). Films and film catalogs sent to or from commercial theaters do not qualify for the Media Mail price."; "Printed music, whether in bound or sheet form."; printed objective test materials and accessories used by or on behalf of educational institutions to test ability, aptitude, achievement, interests and other mental and personal qualities; "Sound recordings, including incidental announcements of recordings and guides or scripts prepared solely for use with such recordings. Video recordings and player piano rolls are classified as sound recordings."; "Playscripts and manuscripts for books, periodicals, and music."; printed educational reference charts ("Each chart must be a single printed sheet of information designed for educational reference", conveyed primarily by graphs, diagrams, tables or other nonnarrative matter; "A chart on which the information is conveyed primarily by textual matter in a narrative form does not qualify"); "Loose-leaf pages and their binders consisting of medical information for distribution to doctors, hospitals, medical schools, and medical students."; "Computer-readable media containing prerecorded information and guides or scripts prepared solely for use with such media." 4.2 books: "Either one envelope or one addressed postcard may be bound into the pages of a book"; one order form; announcements of books incidental and exclusively devoted to books. 6.1 "any printed matter that is mailable as USPS Marketing Mail may be included loose with any qualifying material mailed at the Media Mail or Library Mail prices." 6.2 "Markings that have the character of personal correspondence require, with certain exceptions, additional postage at the First-Class Mail prices." followed by exceptions (names and addresses with From or To and handling directions; marks describing the contents; words or phrases such as "Do Not Open Until Christmas" and "Happy Birthday, Mother."; instructions for use; a manuscript dedication or inscription not having the nature of personal correspondence; marks calling attention to passages; corrections of typographical errors; and others). 6.3 an invoice may be placed inside or in an envelope marked "Invoice Enclosed" attached outside if it relates solely to the matter mailed. Library Mail (5.0): eligible senders and addressees are schools, colleges, universities, public libraries, museums, herbariums and listed nonprofit organizations; not covered by the article.

## Differences shown in the article (not resolved)

- Delivery time: S1 "Shipping in 2-8 Days"; S2 does not guarantee delivery within a specified time. Not a stated conflict: the pages do not say whether the S1 range is an estimate (reworded at Gate 2 round 1).
- Video games and digital media: S1 says video games, computer drives and digital drives do not qualify; S2 lists "computer-readable media containing prerecorded information" and does not mention video games. The article states both and does not say which controls.
- Neither page shows an update date.
