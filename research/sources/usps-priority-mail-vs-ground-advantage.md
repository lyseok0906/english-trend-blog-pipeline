## Differences shown in the article (not resolved)

1. Priority Mail: expected date has no money-back guarantee (explicit). Ground Advantage: silent.
2. Insurance wording: "most shipments (restrictions apply)" against "$100 insurance is included".
3. 108 against 130 inches combined length and girth.
4. Nonstandard fee lists differ (amounts not given); HAZMAT fee listed on Priority Mail, zero on Ground Advantage.
5. Lines on one page only: forwarding/UAA/RTS and "primary option" for ground-only hazmat (Ground Advantage); "no surcharges" line (Priority Mail).
