## S3 — USPS FAQ, "gopost", Article 000007973, dated Oct 22, 2026

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/gopost . 20 of 20 phrase checks.
- Automated, secured, self-service parcel lockers near certain Post Offices, grocery stores, pharmacies, transportation hubs, shopping centers; 24/7 while operational; USPS.com account (free), authentication by ID, account number, card and PIN; no specific locker assigned; address format with account number; 15 days to pick up (5 for Priority Mail Express), then returned; extra services not accepted (Certified Mail, Return Receipt, COD, Registered Mail, Adult Signature, Restricted Delivery, Special Handling).
