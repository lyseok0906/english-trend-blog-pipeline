# Source notes — USPS Registered Mail

Read November 24, 2026.

- USPS [Registered Mail — The Basics](https://faq.usps.com/articles/Knowledge/Registered-Mail-The-Basics), article 000006061. Secure handling, eligible classes, signatures, USPS preparation restriction, expected delivery, tracking limitations, value, insurance and refunds.
- USPS [DMM 503.2.0](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/503.htm): receipts and verification, restricted delivery, declared value, eligible packaging, point of mailing. Sections 2.1 and 2.2 were consulted.

The electronic tracking limitation belongs to Registered Mail alone; do not imply that USPS provides no delivery information. Check packaging conditions before publishing.
