- Most secure service (safes, cages, sealed containers, locks, keys); presented to a retail employee or rural carrier; signature required; Restricted Delivery to the addressee or authorized agent; delivery information when a scan shows delivery or an attempted delivery, none en route; none for APO/FPO/DPO; a system of receipts, not readily available, reached only by starting a claims form (not by phone); four domestic classes; USPS employees may not prepare or seal; resealed items may not be registered; rural carriers may accept with prepaid postage and fees; commercial cash threshold (amount not repeated); delivery date by class, slower, not for speed; declare the full value; insurance matching the declared value up to a maximum (amount not repeated); no postal insurance only if no declared value; PS Form 3806, PS Form 200-N; fee not refundable after acceptance, postage refunded if a claim is made.
