## S1 — USPS, "Standard Forward Mail & Change of Address" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm
- Page date shown: none. Page title: "Standard Forward Mail | USPS".
- Verbatim: "Standard mail forwarding lasts 12 months. You can pay to extend mail forwarding for 6, 12, or 18 more months (18 months is the maximum)." "Although mail forwarding may begin within 3 business days of your submitted request, it's best to allow up to 2 weeks." Mail is forwarded piece by piece. "First-Class Mail and periodicals (newsletters and magazines) are forwarded for free." Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail and USPS Ground Advantage are forwarded for free. Media Mail is forwarded with the customer paying shipping from the local Post Office. "USPS Marketing Mail is not forwarded." "Your change of address order only changes your mailing address with the Post Office." (the page adds that government agencies and companies must still be updated by the customer). Relocating for 15 days up to 1 year: submit a temporary change of address request. Identity must be verified to submit a change of address. "You can't cancel or request a refund for Extended Mail Forwarding." "When your mail forwarding period ends, USPS will return your mail to the sender for 6 months with a label that has your new address." If you will be away for 3-30 days, the page points to Hold Mail.

## S3 — USPS FAQ, "Extended Mail Forwarding" (primary, official)

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Extended-Mail-Forwarding
- Page date shown: "Jul 11, 2026".
- Verbatim: "Extended Mail Forwarding provides customers the ability to extend the forwarding of their mail in 6-, 12-, or 18-month increments, not to exceed a total of 18 months of extended forwarding time. Extended Mail Forwarding is only available for permanent Change of Address. These extensions are in addition to the initial Change of Address forwarding time period of 12 months." "Extended Mail Forwarding is available to all customers requesting a permanent, domestic Change of Address request." Purchase: when submitting the initial request, or with an existing active permanent request using the confirmation code and new ZIP Code (Post Office window, where available a Self-Service Kiosk, or managemymove.usps.com). "An email will be sent to you at the 11th month mark of your COA request reminding you of your option to extend your forwarding." "No, you cannot change your Extended Mail Forwarding request. Once your forwarding begins, your mail will be forwarded according to the duration you selected. You can cancel your COA and that will also cancel your Extended Mail Forwarding request. If canceled, you are not entitled to a refund." Another 6-month interval can be bought "until you have reached an 18 month extension". "once a Change of Address request has expired, you will not be allowed to sign up for Extended Mail Forwarding." Classes that "will continue to be forwarded with Extended Mail Forwarding": First-Class Mail Service, USPS Ground Advantage - Commercial items, Priority Mail Service. The FAQ gives prices (not used).
