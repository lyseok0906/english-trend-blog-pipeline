## S1 — USPS FAQ, "How is Undeliverable and Misdelivered Mail Handled?", Article 000006175, dated May 19, 2026 (primary, official)

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/How-is-Undeliverable-and-Misdelivered-Mail-Handled . 48 of 48 phrase checks matched at once.
- Undeliverable as addressed: the return address is used to try to return it; no return address and incorrectly addressed: local Post Office or the Mail Recovery Center; not arrived within 7 days of mailing: Find Missing Mail, a MissingMail.USPS.com search request, or an online claim for insured items; a domestic piece with a foreign return address: dead mail procedures.
- Apartments: mail is delivered if any one of four elements is present (apartment number, name on or in the receptacle, name on the directory, carrier recognizes it).
- Endorsements (Change Service Requested, Address Service Requested, Return Service Requested, Temp-Return Service Requested); some require an address-correction fee (amount not given and not repeated).
- Postage due: no postage or fell off "Returned for Postage Mail"; shortpaid delivered postage due; refused or undeliverable shortpaid "Returned for Additional Postage"; the "postage due" message stays intact when postage is added; greeting cards without a return address generally delivered postage due in December (domestic First-Class Mail only).
- Mail cannot be picked up before delivery (PO Box, caller service, Delivery Instructions for some packages). Mail to an individual at a business is delivered to the company; USPS responsibility ends; company policy governs; applies to former employees.
- USPS Marketing Mail is not automatically forwarded or returned; handled by the sender's endorsement; no endorsement: disposed of by USPS.
- Misdelivered mail: previous resident, address unknown: collection box or other receptacle with "Not at this address"; Priority Mail Express only: number of the PME Reporting Unit by calling 1-800-ASK-USPS (1-800-275-8777); wrong location: do not erase, mark or endorse; mailbox or mailperson; right location, recipient does not live there: write "Not at this address", do not erase the address, mailperson or collection box; destroying mail may be prohibited by US law, willful destruction may be punishable, questions to local law enforcement; misdelivered "We ReDeliver for You" PS 3849: "Other", write "Refused", mailbox or carrier.

## S2 — USPS FAQ, "Refuse unwanted mail and remove name from mailing lists", Article 000006162, dated Sep 3, 2026

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Refuse-unwanted-mail-and-remove-name-from-mailing-lists . 21 phrase checks, 20 at once (the other one failed only on a parenthesis position and was confirmed in the text).
- Delivered as addressed but "does not have to be accepted"; delivered even if the name differs from the known residents; PO Box mail only to recipients on PS Form 1093; a claimant unknown to the delivery employee may have to show valid photo ID; written request to withhold foreign letter or publication material for up to 2 years; an addressee adding an extra service to a piece being returned must pay return postage as well as the extra service; forwarded when a Change of Address is filed.
- Refusal: cites "Sect. 604.8.1.2 and Sect. 507.1.8.2"; mark "Refused" and return unopened within a reasonable time; opened (even resealed) cannot be marked "Refused" and must go in a new envelope with a correct address and new postage; Registered, Insured, Certified and COD cannot be refused after delivery (customer pays postage); response mail to a promotion not refused when offered; accountable mail can be refused before signing (check "Refused" on the back of the notice, sign by the "X", put it back in the mailbox).
- Mailing lists: DMAchoice (Association of National Advertisers, independent of USPS; a processing fee is mentioned and not repeated) and the credit-reporting companies' prescreen opt-out: not USPS actions, not covered.
