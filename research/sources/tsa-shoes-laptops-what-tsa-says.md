## S1 — TSA national press release, "DHS to End 'Shoes-Off' Travel Policy" (primary, official)

URL https://www.tsa.gov/news/press/releases/2025/07/08/dhs-end-shoes-travel-policy . "National Press Release, Tuesday, July 8, 2025". Subhead: "Passengers can now keep their shoes on at TSA security checkpoints". Verbatim: "WASHINGTON—Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem announced a new policy today which will allow passengers traveling through domestic airports to keep their shoes on while passing through security screening at TSA checkpoints." "Other aspects of TSA's layered security approach will still apply during the TSA checkpoint process. For example, passengers subject must still clear identity verification, Secure Flight vetting, and other processes." The release does not mention an officer's ability to ask for shoes to be removed or additional screening.

## S4 — TSA, Travel Checklist (primary, official)

URL https://www.tsa.gov/travel/travel-tips/travel-checklist (no update date). Under "In Standard Screening Lane": "Remove the 3-1-1 liquids bag and place it in the bin"; "Ensure pockets are empty (keys, tissues, currency, wallets, cell phones, etc.) and remove bulky jewelry (valuable items can be placed in carry-on)"; "Remove personal electronic devices larger than a cell phone from your carry-on bag and place them into a bin with nothing placed on or under them for X-ray screening (E.g. laptops, tablets, e-readers and handheld game consoles.)". Packing tips: "Pack items in layers (shoes one layer, clothes one layer, electronics one layer, etc.)". The only other mention of shoes on the page is that packing tip; no step about removing shoes.

## S6 — TSA, Computed Tomography page (primary, official)

URL https://www.tsa.gov/computed-tomography (no update date). Verbatim (selected): "CT is the latest checkpoint X-ray scanning equipment to enhance threat detection capabilities for carry-on baggage." "The CT technology applies sophisticated algorithms for the detection of explosives and other threats by creating a 3-D image that can be viewed and rotated 360 degrees for a thorough analysis." "In the future, the goal is to keep laptops and 3-1-1 liquids inside of the bag during checkpoint screening. Under current screening procedures for this technology, laptops are allowed to remain inside the bag for screening." No airport list on the page.
