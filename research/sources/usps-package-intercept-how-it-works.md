## S1 — USPS.com, "Package Intercept" (primary, official)

- URL: https://www.usps.com/manage/package-intercept.htm . Title "Package Intercept - Stop Delivery of Letter or Package | USPS". No update date shown (footer "Copyright © 2026 USPS"). Links on the page: price information https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm#_c198 ; "Intercept a Retail Package" https://www.usps.com/packageintercept/welcome.htm .
- Verbatim: "For a fee, USPS Package Intercept® service lets the sender (or authorized representative) stop delivery or redirect a package, letter, or flat that is not out for delivery or already delivered." "Most domestic mailings with a tracking or extra services barcode are eligible for Package Intercept." "Retail Senders: You can redirect a shipment back to your address or to a Post Office™ location as Hold For Pickup. You can only request a Package Intercept online with a USPS.com account." "Commercial Senders: You can redirect a shipment back to your address, to a new delivery address, or to a Post Office location as Hold For Pickup via Business Customer Gateway." "Verify that your shipment is eligible for Package Intercept. If eligible, sign in to or create a USPS.com account to submit your request. Use Retail Package Intercept to request that USPS redirect the shipment back to the original sender (you), hold it at the destination Post Office location, or hold it at a new Post Office location. After submitting your request, you'll be provided an estimated total (intercept fee plus estimated postage, if applicable). We will then attempt to intercept and redirect the shipment." "Intercepted items will be redirected as the service it was originally sent with (Priority Mail Express®, Priority Mail®, USPS Ground Advantage®, or First-Class Mail®). You must pay the applicable postage. Customers will not be charged if the shipment was not intercepted. If successfully intercepted, the non-refundable Package Intercept fee is applied to each intercept request." "Retail and commercial customers can also purchase additional services for the intercepted package, like Adult Signature Required, Adult Signature Restricted Delivery, Signature Confirmation™, and insurance." "USPS Package Intercept is not a guaranteed service and certain conditions apply." What Is Eligible: "Domestic USPS mail services with a USPS Tracking® or extra services barcode" "Packages whose total length and girth length is not larger than 130 inches". What Is Not Eligible: "USPS Marketing Mail® products and periodicals" "Items addressed to a Commercial Mailing Receiving Agency" "Items redirected to a PO Box™" "Nonmailable items, items with surface-only transportation markings, such as Label 127, Surface Mail Only, or items bearing other hazardous materials markings, such as Consumer Commodity ORM-D".
- The page states a fee amount in dollars; **not used**.

## S2 — USPS FAQ, "USPS Package Intercept - The Basics" (primary, official)

- URL: https://faq.usps.com/s/article/USPS-Package-Intercept-The-Basics . Dated "Oct 27, 2026" (Knowledge Article Number 000007877). The sections "Mail Classes and Items Eligible", "How it Works and Time Limits", "How do I Get Started?", "How Much does it Cost?" and "What are Other Options for Intercepted Mail?" are collapsed on the page; the text was read from the page content (textContent).
- Verbatim (selected): "USPS Package Intercept® is a fee-based service that enables the sender or their authorized agent to request that an item that has not been delivered or is not out for delivery to be either: Held for Pickup at the delivery Post Office of the destination address or another Post Office; Returned to Sender; Redirected to a new delivery address. This option is only available for Commercial Package Intercept". "Package Intercept service is available for the following mail classes: Priority Mail Express® Priority Mail® Bound Printed Matter USPS Ground Advantage® - Retail USPS Ground Advantage® - Commercial Parcel Select® Media Mail® Library Mail". "In addition, items must have all the following to be eligible: Have a USPS Tracking® barcode or Extra Services barcode; Be addressed to a domestic recipient (APO/FPO/DPO addresses are excluded); Be addressed from a domestic sender; Not measure more than 130 inches in length and girth combined" (a note on the page adds: only USPS Ground Advantage and Parcel Select oversized-priced items are greater than 108 inches, but not more than 130 inches, in combined length and girth). "A mailpiece is ineligible for Package Intercept if it is any of the following: Sent to or from International destinations; Sent to or from APO/FPO/DPO destinations; A domestic mailpiece requiring a customs declarations label; A domestic mailpiece indicating surface-only transportation such as a mailpiece bearing hazardous materials markings such as "Limited Quantity," "Consumer Commodity" or "ORM-D"; A domestic mailpiece without a tracking barcode (including those for extra services)". "Commercially shipped packages typically contain a tracking number that begins with a "92" or "93" and can only be intercepted by a commercial mailer through the Business Customer Gateway. Package Intercept through USPS.com is intended for retail customers and cannot be used for tracking numbers beginning with a "92" or "93" prefix." "you will need to verify the item is eligible for an intercept using either USPS Tracking® or the eligibility checker on USPS.com®". "Upon request, items are intercepted at the delivery destination Post Office™ and then returned to the sender or Held for Pickup at a Post Office as Hold for Pickup (but not to a PO Box™)." "USPS Package Intercept® requests are active for 7 business days from the date of the request." "The Post Office™ will make every attempt to locate the item during that time. No guarantee that the item can be intercepted before delivery". "As long as the item is not showing as out for delivery or delivered, USPS Package Intercept® can prevent delivery to the addressee if item is located." "You pay all fees ONLY if an intercept was possible." "You cannot edit or cancel your USPS Package Intercept® request. The Package Intercept fee, charged after USPS successfully intercepts the item, is nonrefundable." "If the item is delivered before it can be intercepted, there is no charge." "The USPS Package Intercept fee applies for each mailpiece intercepted." "Items originally mailed as Priority Mail Express®, Priority Mail® and USPS Ground Advantage® being redirected to the sender are not relabeled and bear no additional postage cost." Relabel table (Bound Printed Matter, Media Mail and Library Mail items are relabeled as new Priority Mail items and charged Priority Mail commercial postage, also when redirected to sender; Parcel Select oversized-priced pieces become Ground Advantage items): Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail and USPS Ground Advantage items redirected to a Post Office as Hold for Pickup are relabeled and handled as a new item of the same class at the commercial postage price; Bound Printed Matter, Media Mail, Library Mail and Parcel Select items are relabeled as Priority Mail (Parcel Select oversized-priced pieces as USPS Ground Advantage). "Customers can continue to track the status of the intercepted item using the original article tracking number." Extra services: "Registered Mail® (only when Redirected to Sender)", "Adult Signature Required", "Adult Signature Restricted Delivery", "Insurance (if the original shipment included insurance and only for the original value stated)", "Signature Confirmation™" (the last four when redirected to a new address or to a Post Office for Hold for Pickup). "Registered Mail items are returned with the original Registered Mail number, and are not re-labeled or re-packaged. In addition, customers requesting intercept of Registered Mail pieces must write on the Registered Mail receipt "Withdrawn," sign the receipt, and surrender the receipt to the Post Office™."
- Charges wording that is **not reconciled** (stated as a limit): "These forms of payment will be charged at the time of the intercept or otherwise will be collected from the recipient as postage due upon delivery." "Any applicable postage and fees ... will be charged at the time of intercept – if the intercept is possible – or otherwise will be collected at the time of delivery by a USPS carrier." "the intercept fee is charged at the time of successful intercept, or collected by the USPS carrier at the time of delivery."
- No fee amount is quoted; the FAQ points to "Get USPS Package Intercept price information".

## Where the three sources differ in wording (stated in the article, not resolved)

- Mail classes: S1 names Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, USPS Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail as the services items are redirected as, and excludes Marketing Mail and periodicals; S2 lists seven classes and does not list First-Class Mail; S3 (2016) says all classes except Standard Mail and Periodicals.
- Maximum size: S1 and S2 130 inches in length and girth (S2 has a note that only Ground Advantage and Parcel Select oversized-priced items are over 108 inches and up to 130); S3 108 inches.
- Postage: S1 redirected as the original service; S2 by class with relabeling; S3 Priority Mail rate.
- Charges: S1, S2 and S3 say fees only if intercepted; S2 also has sentences about collection by a carrier at delivery that it does not explain.
