## S2 — USPS Postal Explorer, Domestic Mail Manual 503 "Extra Services", section 3.0 Certified Mail Services (primary, official)

- URL: https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/503.htm
- Page date: **no issue or revision date was found in the page text read** (page footer "Copyright © 2026 USPS"). Cite as "read October 29, 2026". Retrieved as the current DMM page.
- Verbatim anchors (3.1.1): "Certified Mail provides the sender with a mailing receipt and, upon request, electronic verification that an article was delivered or that a delivery attempt was made." "Certified Mail is dispatched and handled in transit as ordinary mail." "Except for Priority Mail pieces with included insurance, no insurance coverage is provided when purchasing Certified Mail." "USPS maintains a record of delivery (which includes the recipient's signature). Customers may obtain a delivery record by purchasing a return receipt (6.0) at the time of mailing."
- 3.2.1: Certified Mail must bear the barcoded green label part of PS Form 3800 (or a non-barcoded green Label 3800-N when a mailer-generated shipping label bearing an IMpb and IMmb is also affixed); placement above the delivery address and to the right of the return address (to the left of the delivery address on parcels); mailer enters the name and complete address on PS Form 3800; postage must cover the Certified Mail fee, the class of mail and any other extra service fees; with a postmarked sender's receipt requested, the clerk round-dates the receipt; otherwise the sender detaches the receipt, marks the date and mails the article.
- 3.2.2 Restricted Delivery: addressee must be an individual (natural person) specified by name; marked "Restricted Delivery" or requested from the clerk; Form 3811 block checked if a return receipt is also requested.
- 1.4.1 Exhibit (read from the page's table): Certified Mail, Certified Mail Restricted Delivery, Certified Mail Adult Signature Required and Adult Signature Restricted Delivery are listed as eligible for **Priority Mail and First-Class Mail**; combinable with Return Receipt (for the Adult Signature services only the Form 3811 option). The "(PM only)" footnote on the Adult Signature rows was **not resolved** from the page text, so the article does not describe Adult Signature beyond S1.
- 1.8: delivery information by article number at www.usps.com or 1-800-222-1811; "A return receipt (hardcopy Form 3811) may be purchased at the time of mailing and is received by mail."

## S3 — USPS.com, "Shipping Insurance and Delivery Services" (primary, official; plain-language page)

- URL: https://www.usps.com/ship/insurance-extra-services.htm
- Page date: none shown in the text read (footer "Copyright © 2026 USPS").
- Verbatim anchor: "Certified Mail®: Prove you sent it. See when it was delivered or that a delivery attempt was made, and get the signature of the person who accepts the mailing when combined with Return Receipt."
