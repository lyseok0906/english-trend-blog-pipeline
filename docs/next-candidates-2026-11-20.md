## Candidate 2: misdelivered USPS mail (held as proposed; reframing suggested)

Source: USPS FAQ "How is Undeliverable and Misdelivered Mail Handled?" (Article 000006175, dated May 19, 2026). Most of the page is about undeliverable-as-addressed mail (return address, Mail Recovery Center, 7 days from mailing, apartment numbers, endorsements, postage due, marketing mail, mail to an individual at a business); the misdelivered part is one short section: mail for a previous resident ("Not at this address" in a collection box), mail delivered to the wrong location (do not mark it; put it back or hand it to the carrier), a "We ReDeliver for You" form for another address ("Other" box, "Refused"), a warning that destroying mail not meant for you may be prohibited, and a Priority Mail Express reporting number. Trends: the misdelivered phrases read 0 to 0.14 x the anchor and "usps package delivered to wrong address" about 0.1 x redelivery, too small for a post of its own; "usps return to sender" read about 0.95 x redelivery over 90 days (0.63 x in the last 28 days).

Proposed reframing (needs the user's decision): "USPS undeliverable mail and returned mail: what happens and what to do with mail that is not yours", with the sender side (undeliverable as addressed, return address, no return address, Missing Mail after 7 days, postage due, marketing mail) and the receiver side (previous resident, wrong location, wrong redelivery notice) kept apart. Overlap to check at drafting: Missing Mail post (search steps), Mail Forwarding post (forwarding of mail for movers), tracking status post (Forwarded, return statuses). Not drafted.
