## Candidate 1: USPS Smart Lockers (approved by the user and drafted 2026-11-24)

Reframed after the extra Trends comparisons: "usps parcel locker" reads far higher than "usps smart locker", and on USPS pages "parcel locker" can mean a gopost unit or a cluster box locker, so the article is built on the phrase and separates three kinds of locker. The index is not read as Smart Locker interest alone; search volume not checked. Draft: `content/drafts/usps-smart-locker-vs-parcel-locker.md` (backlog row 42).

Source read 2026-11-24: USPS FAQ "USPS Smart Locker" (Article 000008414, dated Jul 28, 2026): what the lockers are, pickup by one-time QR code or access code, held 5 calendar days in the locker then 10 more days at the retail counter, After Hours Access, shipping to a locker (Click-N-Ship, Self-Service Kiosk), redelivery to a locker, Informed Delivery Daily Digest QR code, returns from a locker (Label Broker QR code), USPS Local XChange. Still to read before drafting: usps.com Smart Lockers pages (package recipients, Local XChange) and the FAQ "What is a Cluster Box? What is a Parcel Locker?" (to keep the two kinds of lockers apart).
Overlap: the redelivery post (a Smart Locker is one redelivery choice), the PO Box post (oversized PO Box packages go to a locker, one sentence), Informed Delivery. No dedicated post.
