## Candidate A: USPS Ground Advantage and Priority Mail compared (what each service includes)

Reader problem: which service to pick for a package, how long each is listed to take, what is included (tracking, insurance, pickup), and the size and weight limits. Official sources, opened 2026-11-17 in the built-in browser: USPS.com "Ground Advantage" (https://www.usps.com/ship/ground-advantage.htm) and "Priority Mail" (https://www.usps.com/ship/priority-mail.htm), plus USPS.com "Shipping Insurance and Delivery Services" (https://www.usps.com/ship/insurance-extra-services.htm). No update dates shown. Facts seen: Ground Advantage "2-5 days" with tracking included, includes forwarding, UAA and return to sender, free package pickup, $100 insurance included (extra up to $5,000), maximum 70 lbs, combined length and girth 130 inches, ground-only HAZMAT; Priority Mail "2-3 Days", tracking and up to $100 insurance with most shipments (restrictions apply), no fuel, residential, rural or Saturday surcharges, Flat Rate option, 70 lbs, 108 inches. Differences worth showing without prices: 2-3 against 2-5 days, 108 against 130 inches, "$100 included" against "up to $100 with most shipments (restrictions apply)". Prices and fees are all left out. Open point: the footnotes behind "2-3 Days" and "2-5 days" were not located on the page text; read at Gate 1. Overlap: no live post explains the two services; incidental mentions only (holiday deadlines post uses both names for dates; package pickup, intercept and certified mail posts mention them). Today: feasible; sources opened.

## Candidate B: USPS claims (who can file, deadlines, documents), with included insurance in brief

Reader problem: a package is lost or damaged; who can file, by when, and what to bring. Official sources: USPS.com "File a Claim" (https://www.usps.com/help/claims.htm; no update date), opened 2026-11-17: sender or recipient may file; original mailing receipt needed; damaged or missing contents: file immediately, no later than 60 days after the mailing date; lost: a filing window per service (a table of "file after / file before" periods by service: Priority Mail Express 7 days, Priority Mail, Ground Advantage, Insured Mail and Registered Mail 15 days, each before 60 days; military and APO services have longer periods); documents: tracking or label number, evidence of insurance, proof of value (a list), proof of damage; uninsured items may not be paid and a Missing Mail Search is the alternative. Also USPS.com "Shipping Insurance and Delivery Services" (included insurance and the 60-day sentence). Overlap: the published Missing Mail post covers the search and gives only the two filing cases, explicitly without the per-service table, and points to this claims page; the published Certified Mail post says insurance is not included with Certified Mail. The new post would deepen the claims side and link to both; partial overlap, stated. Today: feasible; sources opened. Claim amounts and insurance fees would be left out (coverage limits of $100 and $5,000 are stated as limits, not prices).
