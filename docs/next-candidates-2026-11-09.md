## What was and was not measured

Google Trends (US, past 12 months to 2026-11-09, weekly values, 0 to 100, scaled to the highest point inside each comparison of up to five phrases) was read through the explore and widget-data endpoints from a Trends page in the built-in browser. Every comparison contained the anchor phrase "can you bring a vape on a plane" (average 68.7 when compared with small phrases; much lower when compared with large USPS phrases), so each phrase is also shown as a multiple of the anchor inside the same comparison ("x vape"). These are **relative interest indexes, not search volume**. No tool in this setup gives search volume (no keyword-volume tool, no Search Console data for topics without a page), so **search volume was not checked** for any candidate. Question-form phrases ("how does usps package pickup work", "do you have to take your shoes off at tsa") read zero or near zero in Trends, which at this scale means low interest in that exact wording, not necessarily no interest.

## Why these two

- **TSA, shoes and laptops by lane.** The shoes phrases are among the highest-interest TSA phrases measured in this survey and the laptop phrase is also high; the question is practical. The sources are all official and current: a dated national release (July 8, 2025), two item pages updated in July 2026, the Travel Checklist, the CT page and a dated local release (July 13, 2026). It does not overlap a live post in content (the PreCheck post covers the PreCheck lane with one FAQ sentence about the standard lane; the 3-1-1 post covers liquids). A real source tension (the July 2025 release against the PreCheck-only shoe sentence still on item pages) is shown without being resolved.
- **USPS Package Pickup.** "usps package pickup" is about 2.9 times and "usps schedule pickup" about 4.1 times the anchor, the latter rising. One official FAQ answers nearly every reader question (cost, cutoff, eligible mail, limits, postage, scheduling, what to do if it fails); a second USPS.com page confirms the free pickup and the 10 oz and 1/2 inch stamp rule. No prices are needed. No overlap with live posts (Package Intercept, Hold Mail, Media Mail and Forwarding are different services; Media Mail is linked because it is not named on the eligible list).
